## Description

**What happened**:

JobSet's Job foreground deletion was blocked by the stuck "Failed" Pod.

**What you expected to happen**:

Forcefully delete such a pod when the mechanism kueue.x-k8s.io/safe-to-forcefully-delete is enabled.

**How to reproduce it (as minimally and precisely as possible)**:

This seems like a tricky race condition. 

In our environment the Kubelet was unhealty. It seems it transitioned the Pod into the Failed phase, but didn't finalize with the forceful delete request at the end.
