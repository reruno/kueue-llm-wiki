### Comment by [@ivnovakov](https://github.com/ivnovakov) — 2026-05-28T19:06:21Z

Hi, sure!

After looking at the prow logs, the error is most likely a transient 404 when downloading a github release asset.
The same url works fine shortly after.

I'd suggest retries like we do in `e2e_docker_pull_if_needed` (7 attempts, exponential backoff) for manifest downloads .
A small helper that retries `curl -fsSL` and pipes into `kubectl apply --server-side -f -`, reusing `hack/retry.sh` would work in this case. 
I'd also add `--exponential` flag to `retry.sh` to support backoff.

We could also introduce this behavior for `PROMETHEUS_OPERATOR_BUNDLE`, `JOBSET_MANIFEST`, `LEADERWORKERSET_MANIFEST`, and `CERTMANAGER_MANIFEST`.

WDYT?
