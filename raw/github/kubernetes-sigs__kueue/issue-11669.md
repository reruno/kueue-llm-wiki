**What would you like to be cleaned**:

I would like to optimize pull-kueue-test-e2e-sequential-baseline-main by splitting into two shards.

I think the splitting could be done by the Describe-level annotations like "shard-1", "shard-2".

Then we will have two CI jobs, one per shard.

**Why is this needed**:

- This is the longest taking CI job currently, 24min, by far: https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/issues/11606#issuecomment-4548333911
- we have exhausted other optimization ideas in the issue: https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/issues/11274
