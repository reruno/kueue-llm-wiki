### Comment by [@apullo777](https://github.com/apullo777) — 2026-06-10T19:54:18Z

Hi, I'd like to work on this :)
 
I pulled a few recent runs of periodic-kueue-test-e2e-multikueue-extended-main from Prow. They average ~20 min (range 18.9–22.6 over the last 10 runs). The bulk of that is cluster/operator setup, not test execution: 

Ginkgo itself only runs ~3 min wall-clock (Ran 10 of 10 Specs in 179.86s, 5 parallel nodes), while building the kind image, bringing up the 3 kind clusters, and pulling/loading the 6 external operators (AppWrapper, JobSet, training-operator, Kubeflow Trainer, MPI, KubeRay) eats the other ~16 min. Within that ~3 min of specs, the Ray group (RayJob/RayCluster/RayService) is the heaviest: ~164s of ~258s serial spec time (63%), and it's the only group that needs KubeRay + the Ray image. 

So my thought is to isolate Ray into its own shard by label, mirroring how sequential-extended was sharded (feature-label + per-shard conditional operator install), so each shard only installs the operators it actually needs: 
- shard-0: LeaderWorkerSet, JobSet, AppWrapper, Kubeflow (PyTorchJob / MPIJob / TrainJob)
- shard-1: the Ray integration tests
