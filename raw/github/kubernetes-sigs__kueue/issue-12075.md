### Comment by [@apullo777](https://github.com/apullo777) — 2026-06-12T12:44:13Z

I agree that 10s is tight for MultiKueue admission in general, since it often includes cross-cluster round trips. Happy to follow up with a PR increasing the timeout for the MK e2e waits.

I checked the `test/e2e/multikueue/` usages of `util.Timeout`: they are `Eventually`-style waits, so increasing the timeout should not slow down the happy path. Since both worker-side checks and manager-side admission checks can involve the full manager→worker→manager flow, I do not think selecting only “cross-cluster” call sites gives us a clean boundary.

I would propose adding a dedicated constant, for example `MultiKueueTimeout = 30s`, and replacing util.Timeout across the MultiKueue suites. That keeps the diff mechanical and gives new MultiKueue tests a safer default. Reusing `MediumTimeout` would also work if we prefer not to add another constant.
