## Description

## What would you like to be added?
When Kueue needs to reclaim resources from a running `RayService`, it should be able to scale down worker groups rather than fully suspending the service.

## Why is this needed?
A common pattern is to run a long-lived `RayService` that borrows idle GPUs on the cluster to maximize utilization. When higher-priority training jobs arrive and need those GPUs back, the service should scale down to its `minReplicas` and remain available, rather than being fully terminated.

Consider a `RayService` with `minReplicas=1, maxReplicas=10` in a queue with 1 unit of nominal quota and 9 units of borrow limit. When the service is running at full capacity (borrowing 9 units) and a high-priority training job is admitted, preemption today suspends the entire service. The expected behavior is to scale it down to `minReplicas=1`, freeing the borrowed quota while keeping the service available.
