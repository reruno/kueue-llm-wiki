## Description

## What would you like to be added?
When Kueue needs to reclaim resources from a running `RayService`, it should be able to scale down worker groups rather than fully suspending the service.

## Why is this needed?
A common pattern is to run a long-lived `RayService` that borrows idle GPUs on the cluster to maximize utilization. When higher-priority training jobs arrive and need those GPUs back, the service should scale down to its `minReplicas` and remain available, rather than being fully terminated.

Consider a `RayService` with `minReplicas=1, maxReplicas=10` in a queue with 1 unit of nominal quota and 9 units of borrow limit. When the service is running at full capacity (borrowing 9 units) and a high-priority training job is admitted, preemption today suspends the entire service. The expected behavior is to scale it down to `minReplicas=1`, freeing the borrowed quota while keeping the service available.

### Comment by [@yaroslava-serdiuk](https://github.com/yaroslava-serdiuk) — 2026-08-03T14:23:56Z

I see. Let's specify the exact use cases so we can add them later to our e2e test suite. By checking these scenarios, we can ensure we are aligned on the expected preemption and scaling behaviors.

**ClusterQueue (CQ) Base Configuration (Applies to all cases):**

* **CQ A:** Nominal quota = 1, Borrowing limit = 9
* **CQ B:** Nominal quota = 9, Borrowing limit = 1

### Use Case 1: Partial Preemption / Scale Down

* **Initial State:** A low-priority `RayService/RayCluster` is admitted in CQ A, consuming a total quota of 10 (1 nominal + 9 borrowed from CQ B).
* **Trigger:** A high-priority `Job X` is submitted to CQ B, requiring a quota of 9.
* **Expected Behavior:** The `RayService` in CQ A is partially preempted (scaled down) to use only its nominal quota of 1. `Job X` is fully admitted in CQ B, consuming its own nominal quota of 9.

### Use Case 2: Full Preemption

* **Initial State:** A low-priority `RayService/RayCluster` is admitted in CQ A, consuming a total quota of 10 (1 nominal + 9 borrowed from CQ B).
* **Trigger:** A high-priority `Job X` is submitted to CQ B, requiring a quota of 10.
* **Expected Behavior:** The `RayService` in CQ A is fully preempted (evicted) to make room for `Job X`. `Job X` is fully admitted in CQ B (using its 9 nominal + 1 borrowed from CQ A).

### Use Case 3: Prioritizing Full Preemption Over Widespread Scale-Down

* **Initial State:** Two low-priority workloads are admitted in CQ A:
* `RayService A`: Consuming 5 quota (1 nominal + 4 borrowed from CQ B).
* `RayService B`: Consuming 5 quota (0 nominal + 5 borrowed from CQ B).


* **Trigger:** A high-priority `Job X` is submitted to CQ B, requiring a quota of 5.
* **Expected Behavior:** `RayService B` is fully preempted to make room for `Job X` to run. Kueue should *not* attempt to partially downscale both workloads (e.g., downscaling `RayService B` to 1 and `RayService A` to 4). One workload is cleanly preempted to satisfy the high-priority request.
