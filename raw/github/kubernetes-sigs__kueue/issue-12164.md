### Comment by [@andrewseif](https://github.com/andrewseif) — 2026-06-16T10:29:59Z

My initial investigation shows that this is indeed a bug.
and these are my initial purposed fixes:

Fix 1 (minimal): reorder update Manager/cache labels before clearing metrics.
Do` r.queues.UpdateLocalQueue` / `r.cache.UpdateLocalQueue` first, then `clearLocalQueueMetrics`. Once the Manager's cached labels are non-matching, `heads()` short-circuits and can't repopulate the series, so the final clear sticks. Both `UpdateLocalQueue` and `heads() `hold `m.Lock()`, so they're serialized; only the clear is lock-free, and after the reorder it runs when no writer can re-set the series. Small, low-risk.

Fix 2 (most robust): move the matching→non-matching clear into the Manager, under `m.Lock().`
Detect the transition inside `Manager.UpdateLocalQueue` (manager.go:534) and call `clearLQMetrics` there atomically with `qImpl.update(q).` This makes "update cached labels" and "clear metrics" a single critical section, eliminating the window entirely and keeping the label/metric invariant consistent. Slightly larger change but removes the class of bug rather than just this ordering.

Not sufficient on its own: a test-only fix. Wrapping in Eventually (already done) or adding a workload "nudge" can't help, because once the series is stuck nothing re-clears it. The fix has to be in the controller/Manager.

My recommendation: Fix 1 as the targeted bugfix for this issue (smallest change that closes the race), and consider Fix 2 as a follow-up hardening in a separate ticket that I can work on.

### Comment by [@mszadkow](https://github.com/mszadkow) — 2026-06-16T10:32:09Z

I did the investigation too.
Here is the order of events:
```
07:32:46.952 - LocalQueue reconcile conflict on queue-delete-metrics (object modified)

07:32:47.040 - Second LocalQueue reconcile conflict on the same queue-delete-metrics during the label-change window

07:32:47.042 - Test enters “Verifying metrics are cleared after labels are updated to non-matching”

07:32:57.044 - Eventually times out; spec fails at the assertion in localqueue_controller_test.go:720
 pending_workloads status=active stayed 1 instead of 0 after 10.001s
```

We have two event sources touching the same LocalQueue time window:
- LocalQueue label update events
- Workload-driven queue metric updates
Both can lead to metric writes. The test expects a transition from visible to hidden metrics after label change.

Possible solution:

Add extra clear at the end of Update as a deterministic cleanup pass for that transition, after queue/cache updates, to wipe any stale republish that slipped in during the conflict window.
That clear is the safety net. 
If any stale series got recreated during concurrent reconciles, it is removed again after updates settle.
