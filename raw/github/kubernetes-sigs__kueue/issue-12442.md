I took a quick look at the Schedule-style run loops in `pkg/scheduler/`. My read is that they split naturally into separate groups: the core scheduling tests, the TAS-specific tests, and possibly AFS

To keep the refactor reviewable, I would handle these in separate PRs. Strting with the core group first:`TestSchedule`, `TestScheduleForFairSharing`, and `TestScheduleConcurrentAdmission`, with a shared `scheduleTestCase` and `runScheduleTestCases` helper.

### Comment by [@apullo777](https://github.com/apullo777) — 2026-06-25T05:29:47Z

I checked the remaining bespoke `t.Run` loops in `pkg/scheduler/*_test.go`, and I do not think further commonization would improve readability. 

The remaining tests each have test-specific setup or scheduling flow, such as TAS admission/resource handling, AFS-specific cache setup, multi-iteration scheduling, concurrent-update interceptors, or single-scenario assertions.

Trying to fold those into the shared helpers would likely require extra knobs and branches, which may go against the goal of this cleanup.

So from my side, I think the high-value commonization is covered. Please feel free to close this if that matches your expectation, or let me know if there is another specific test you would still like me to look at :)
