## Description

**What would you like to be added**:

With `SchedulingEquivalenceHashing` enabled, we get a notion of workload "classes" that are currently pending in the Kueue, i.e. unique requirements for the workloads.

We could add a metric that would expose how many unique hashes are pending in a queue to see how many of those classes the queue has to consider.

**Why is this needed**:

By dividing the indamissble requeue time _T1_ by the average duration of the scheduling cycle _S1_ we get a notion of more or less how many cycles the scheduler can execute before the inadmissible workloads are requeued.

Without `SchedulingEquivalenceHashing`, we can pit it against the number of pending workloads when trying to estimate whether workloads lower down in the queue are getting starved when the top workloads are inadmissible (i.e. no progress is made in the queue).

With `SchedulingEquivalenceHashing` enabled, the number to compare would have to be the number of pending hashes, since they are moved to inadmissible in bulk.
