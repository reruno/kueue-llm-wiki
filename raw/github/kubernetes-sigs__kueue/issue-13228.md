**What would you like to be cleaned**:
Our review skill needs better routing where the orchestrating agent can be directed to use a subset of the skill. Currently it eats up tokens. Maybe make the agent prompt a menu to ask which areas you want it to review.

Specifically, during the first review pass, I just care about high level architectural issues. Once those are fixed, then I can go deeper into style/security/etc. 

**Why is this needed**:
Stop gobbling my tokens 🦃 Also want the review skill accessible to contributors with free versions of agents rather than limited to people who work at employers who give large token budgets 😂 

Then I feel comfortable pointing coderabbit at the review skill. We have coderabbit rate limits and don't want to ratelimit ourselves.
