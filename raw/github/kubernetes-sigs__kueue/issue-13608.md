### Comment by [@MichalZylinski](https://github.com/MichalZylinski) — 2026-07-29T06:17:55Z

The reasoning behind that decision was simple: the former 'Talks & Presentations' section was renamed to 'Talks & Case Studies' to consolidate all relevant content scattered across the web, which still requires manual curation. Meanwhile, the Medium news feed operates on a self-service basis to keep the site active. While we may treat featured content differently, its inherent size constraints mean we'll need clear guidelines—such as how to select three items from our growing library and how long to promote them.

### Comment by [@MichalZylinski](https://github.com/MichalZylinski) — 2026-07-30T09:40:56Z

I'm not fully convinced that showing the latest 3 case studies indefinitely is the right approach here. Since the page has just been divided between contributor (Join Us) and case studies (User Showcase), relying only on case studies means we lose a safe spot for plenty of external content like news and tutorials (how-to articles, Kubecon recordings, new release announcements, etc.).

What I'd ideally like to see featured in the user showcase (or preferably on the home page) are hand-picked customer logos (similar to ray.io or kubeflow.org). I understand the approval process will be rigid, but we could treat it as a one-off, targeted effort.
