## Description

**What would you like to be added**:

An umbrella for in-flight scheduling changes that share one root cause: the scheduling cycle is built on assumptions that break under load. The snapshot goes stale as the cycle runs, decisions can't be revisited once made, and cross-cycle progress is rebuilt from scratch every cycle. On busy clusters this shows up as unfair ordering, starved ClusterQueues, and workloads pinned to flavors that can never admit them.

**Work items:**

- [ ] 1. Mid-cycle DRS recomputation and re-ordering (+ refill)
- [ ] 2. Overlapping preemption targets
- [ ] 3. TAS-aware flavor selection
- [ ] 4. Cross-cycle flavor progress

### Work item 1: Mid-cycle DRS recomputation and re-ordering (+ refill)

The cycle can't reprice a turn already won: a workload that changes cost mid-cycle (e.g. a flavor switch during TAS recompute) keeps the position it won at the old price, and an admission doesn't let the winning CQ's next workload join the running cycle. Proposal: recompute DRS and re-run the ordering mid-cycle. Consumers:

- #13691: TAS flavor fallback repricing
- Refill: https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/13496#issuecomment-5126216451, prototype/benchmark in progress
- #9345: motivating fairness bug

### Work item 2: Overlapping preemption targets

Two preemptors in one cycle can claim the same victims; the second's assignment is computed against capacity the first already spoke for. Punted from #13496 to a standalone KEP. Prior art and lessons: #6488, #6541, KEP #6776, #6581, summarized in https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/issues/13320#issuecomment-5100495192.

Same failure family (preempted capacity misaccounted):

- #12538: double subtraction of preempted workload-slice usage
- #10497: TAS preemption under-selects the victims needed to free a topology domain
- Sorta related: #13320, `FairSharingPrioritizeNonBorrowing` ignoring leaf-CQ non-borrowing at higher tournament levels

### Work item 3: TAS-aware flavor selection

Flavors are picked on quota alone; topology feasibility is discovered only after the flavor is locked in, with no feedback to the flavor iterator. Root cause of #13658 (pinned to a flavor whose topology can't fit) and #10824 (pinned to a second flavor instead of preempting on the first). The #12419 stickiness and the `TASFlavorFallback` gate (#13691) are workarounds. Note: the #13691 gate is reachable only when nomination-time placement succeeded and then broke mid-cycle; nomination-time TAS failures (the #13658 fragmentation reproducer) never trigger the recompute, which is why work item 4's placement-failure record is the near-term cover for that case. Proposal (sketched in #10824): feed the TAS result into the per-flavor Fit/Preempt/NoFit decision, using a cheap conservative feasibility check for performance. Landing this retires the workarounds.

Same failure family (flavor ordering without an accurate admissibility verdict):

- #13607: `PreemptionOverBorrowing` ranks a flavor where admission is impossible above one already evaluated as Fit

### Work item 4: Cross-cycle flavor progress

Two bugs plus a missing record. (a) `LastTriedFlavorIdx` (the "which flavor to try next" memory) is erased on any `AllocatableResourceGeneration` tick, i.e. every cycle on a busy cluster, so `TryNextFlavor` never advances. (b) Even if kept, the bookmark only marks where the next quota scan starts; under `whenCanBorrow: TryNextFlavor` with borrowing-based fit the scan covers every flavor and records "start over" (`flavorassigner.go:1104`), so preserving it alone doesn't help. (c) The post-pick TAS placement verdict is recorded nowhere, so a flavor that failed placement is back on the ballot next cycle at full preference. Second half of #13658.

Proposal, near-term (leading candidate per discussion in #13691): record placement-failed flavors per workload at nomination time and skip them in the flavor iterator, invalidated on TAS-relevant change or TTL rather than generation churn (variant of #13658 fix idea 3). Selective invalidation of `LastTriedFlavorIdx` (erase only when the change could make an already-tried flavor fit, without reverting #2984) remains the complementary fix for non-TAS fungibility. Once work item 3 lands, the placement-failure record can retire for TAS.

Also tracking, same pattern elsewhere:

- #10060 (queue-manager cache): `noFitSchedulingHashes` cleared wholesale every requeue cycle
- #8653 (requeue triggers): the inverse, a relevant event (non-TAS workload finishing) that should trigger requeue and doesn't

**Why is this needed**:

These failures are load-proportional: they surface late as production incidents (#12419, #13658). They're being discussed across at least four threads (#13691, #13496, #13320, #10824). One tracking point shows how the pieces compose.
