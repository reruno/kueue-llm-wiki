## Description

### What would you like to be added?

A new Prometheus gauge metric `kueue_cohort_subtree_pending_workloads` that reports the number of pending workloads per cohort subtree, broken down by status (active or inadmissible).

### Why is this needed?

Kueue currently reports pending workload counts per ClusterQueue and quota rollups per Cohort, but there's no metric showing pending workloads aggregated across a Cohort's subtree. This makes it hard to answer capacity questions at the team or department level without manually summing across every ClusterQueue.
