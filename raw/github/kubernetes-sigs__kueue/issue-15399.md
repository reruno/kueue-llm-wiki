## Description

**What happened**:

When an elastic job with `elastic-job-scale-up-strategy: partial` is evicted while a scale-up probe is pending, the job loses all admission and cannot recover, even though the quota that held it is free again.

The eviction finishes the slice that held the admitted replicas, and the only surviving slice is the scale-up probe. 
That probe's `minCount` was computed as *the count admitted when it was created + 1*, which by construction does not fit in the quota available at that time. 
So the probe reports `QuotaReserved=False, reason=ExceedsMaxQuota` indefinitely and the job runs nothing.

Observed with a drained ClusterQueue (`stopPolicy: HoldAndDrain`), but the cause is in the shared slice-normalization path, so any eviction of the partially-admitted slice (preemption included) should reach the same state.

Sequence, from a run against a 7-pod ClusterQueue:

1. RayCluster admitted at 5 workers (6 pods).
2. Scaled to 10 workers. New slice, `minCount` 6, partially admitted at 6 workers (7 pods).
3. Scale-up probe created for the full 10, `minCount` 7 — pending, since 8 pods exceeds the quota.
4. ClusterQueue drained. The partially-admitted slice is evicted with `WorkloadEvictedByClusterQueueStopped`.
5. `normalizeActiveSlices` skips it as evicted, so `latestWithQuotaReservation` and `pendingReplacement` are both nil and `latestNonEvicted` is the probe. The evicted slice is finished, releasing its quota.
6. ClusterQueue resumed. The probe is the job's only slice, its floor of 8 pods still exceeds the 7-pod quota, and the job has nothing admitted. Usage stays at 0.

**What you expected to happen**:

Once the eviction clears, the job is re-admitted at the count it held before - 6 workers, 7 pods - with the scale-up probe still pending for the full 10.
That is the state the job was in before the drain, and it is what already happens for an elastic job that is *not* mid scale-up: see the batch/Job integration spec "Should resume with the same slice chain after eviction by a stopped ClusterQueue".
A job should not become permanently unschedulable because it happened to be mid scale-up when it was evicted.

**How to reproduce it (as minimally and precisely as possible)**:

Enable `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlices` and `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlicesWithPartialReplicaScaleUp`, then:

1. Create a ClusterQueue with `pods: 7`.
2. Create a RayCluster annotated `kueue.x-k8s.io/elastic-job: "true"` and `kueue.x-k8s.io/elastic-job-scale-up-strategy: partial`, with 1 head and 5 workers, each worker requesting 1 pod's worth of quota.
3. Once admitted, scale the worker group to 10. The job is partially admitted at 6 workers and a scale-up probe is created for the full 10.
4. Set `stopPolicy: HoldAndDrain` on the ClusterQueue, wait for the eviction, then set it back to `None`.
5. The job has no admitted slice, and the surviving probe reports `ExceedsMaxQuota`.

**Anything else we need to know?**:

**Suggested fix.** Treat an evicted slice as dormant rather than dead. `normalizeActiveSlices` currently finishes every evicted slice, on the grounds that they hold quota that must be released. When the evicted slice is the only one holding the job's admission, finishing it discards the job's admitted size and leaves behind a probe whose floor no live admission supports. Instead, leave that slice to be requeued and re-admitted, keeping the replacement chain intact: the probe then stays pending against a floor that is meaningful again once the slice is back.

This keeps partial scale-up consistent with the atomic case rather than giving it separate semantics, and it avoids having to invent a rule for recomputing a stale floor — a rule the KEP does not specify, and for which none of the candidates are good (dropping `minCount` makes the workload all-or-nothing and forfeits partial admission; `minCount = 1` is arbitrary; `minCount = minReplicas` contradicts the KEP, which deliberately does not use `minReplicas`).

Constraint for whoever picks this up: finishing evicted slices unconditionally was introduced by #11327 to stop duplicate replacement slices getting stuck during rapid scaling. That scenario has to keep working — its table-driven test is in `pkg/workloadslicing/workloadslicing_test.go`, around the `normalizeActiveSlices` cases.

Other notes:

- Two things recover the job today, both requiring action from outside the job: raising the ClusterQueue's quota to cover the probe's floor, or scaling the job's replicas back down (the probe holds no quota reservation, so `EnsureWorkloadSlices` rewrites its counts in place). The first is verified by an integration test; the second is inferred from the code and has not been run.
- Separate but noticed while investigating: the evicted slice keeps stale `QuotaReserved=True` and `Admitted=True` conditions alongside `Evicted=True` and `Finished=True`. Worth filing on its own, and worth checking early here — a fix that decides "is this slice still holding the job's admission?" by reading those conditions would read stale state.
- The floor is frozen at slice creation in `pkg/controller/jobframework/reconciler.go` (`minCount := prevAdmittedCount + 1`) and never recomputed, which is why it can outlive the slice that gave it meaning.
- Relevant code: `normalizeActiveSlices` in `pkg/workloadslicing/workloadslicing.go`.
- Part of KEP-12100 (Partial Replica ScaleUp for ElasticJob), built on KEP-77.
