## Description

Our team is keen on enabling and using Kueue's new concurrent admission feature. However, we see adoption risk from one of the stated limitations of the current implementation documented on the [Concurrent admission](https://kueue.sigs.k8s.io/docs/concepts/concurrent_admission/) page:

> The `resourceGroup` can contain at most 16 ResourceFlavors.

Is there a specific reason why 16 was chosen as the limit, and is it  possible that this limit can be raised in the future? In our use case we are looking at enabling this feature on queues with up 14 different defined resource flavors, which doesn't give us a lot of headroom for additional resource flavors we may want to introduce in the future.

I think a limit of 32 would be great, but even a small bump in the limit to e.g. 24 or so would be helpful for adoption.

I should note also that we don't actually intend to concurrently admit on all of our resource flavors. We are looking at doing so on at most three at a time currently.

### Comment by [@ResidentMario](https://github.com/ResidentMario) — 2026-09-11T20:29:18Z

> One detail that would help assess the impact: when you say “at most three at a time,” is that per workload, and what determines those three flavors?

The three flavors are capacity source variants. We have two tiers of reservation-backed node pools (each of which gets its own resource flavor) per GPU family (e.g. L4, A100, etcetera) and a fallback to on-demand (DWS, which is the third resource flavor).

Concurrent admission is attractive because it allows us to promote jobs waiting on on-demand capacity up onto reservation-backed capacity that is freshly done running some other workload, which we expect will help us use our fleet of GPU reservations more efficiently and help tamp down on job scheduling times at the edge (p90 plus).

### Comment by [@sohankunkerkar](https://github.com/sohankunkerkar) — 2026-09-11T20:42:01Z

Thanks, that helps explain the need for more flavors. You have several GPU families, but each job only needs to try a few capacity sources. I support increasing the limit to 32. It gives you more room, but Kueue will still create a variant for every configured flavor. We haven’t yet tested the performance impact at your scale.                    

A useful test would be whether a job waiting on DWS can promptly use reservation capacity when it becomes available. Your setup also gives us a concrete example for the KEP’s planned support for explicitly choosing which variants to create.
