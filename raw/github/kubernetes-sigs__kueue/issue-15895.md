### Comment by [@apullo777](https://github.com/apullo777) — 2026-09-21T15:39:21Z

This looks like #14903 resurfacing through a different logger path.

#14908/#14913 discarded the simulator's construction logger, but the racing reflectors here carry `schedulingCycle=1`, so that fix does not cover this path. Rather than re-parenting another call site, I think the cleaner fix is to switch `utiltesting.NewLogger` from `testr` to `ktesting`.

With no simulator change, a local reproduction on `release-0.19` goes from 4 races to 0 under `-race -count 3`.

I also found two separate lifecycle issues that I would keep apart: `main` does not wait for the per-snapshot WAS informer factory to stop, and scheduler-library starts its long-lived shared factory with a per-snapshot context.

I'm planning to send the logger fix on `main`, with the WAS shutdown as a separate preventive PR. Happy to split it differently if you prefer.
