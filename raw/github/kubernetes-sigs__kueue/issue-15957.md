## Description

**What would you like to be cleaned**:

`calculateLendable` is recomputed once per preemption candidate during fair sharing, and the result cannot change between those calls.

`dominantResourceShare` calls `calculateLendable` on every invocation. The fair-sharing tournament reaches it once per candidate through `ComputeTargetShareAfterRemoval`, from both `(*CohortSnapshot).DominantResourceShare` and `(*ClusterQueueSnapshot).DominantResourceShare`. Each call walks to the Cohort root and sums `potentialAvailable` over every FlavorResource, recursing back up the tree for each one, and allocates a fresh result map.

Nothing it reads changes during the tournament. `potentialAvailable` reads only `SubtreeQuota`, `localQuota` and `BorrowingLimit`. It never reads `Usage`, and `Usage` is the only thing the tournament mutates.

**Why is this needed**:

On two production clusters running preemption-heavy fair-sharing workloads, `calculateLendable` is 11.5% and 11.7% of sampled CPU, measured from 3 x 60s profiles per cluster on v0.19.3. Every call arrives from `dominantResourceShare`. Roughly 48% of it is map work: `mapassign_faststr` 21%, `makemap` 8.6% for the per-call result map, and the rest iteration and lookup. `potentialAvailable` is 47%.

The busier of the two clusters, re-rooted at `fairPreemptions`:

![Fair-sharing CPU flamegraph](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/venuchitta/kueue/pprof-assets/flame-lendable.png)

`fairPreemptions` is 47.1% of sampled CPU there. The two pink towers are the two entry points into `dominantResourceShare`. Orange is the map and allocator work underneath.

#15955 maintains it per Cohort alongside `SubtreeQuota`. `BenchmarkRunFirstFsStrategy` shows -20% ns/op and -38% B/op at 10 candidates per ClusterQueue, and -15% / -34% at 100. Its cohort tree is depth 1 with a single FlavorResource, so it mostly measures the removed per-call map allocation rather than the walk.
