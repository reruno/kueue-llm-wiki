## Description

**What happened**:
During an elastic partial replica scale-up, `prepareWorkloadSliceForScaleUp` (`pkg/controller/jobframework/reconciler.go`) copies a matched PodSet's `minCount` forward from its predecessor Workload's own recorded value, with no check against the PodSet's own new `count`.
When one PodSet shrinks while a different PodSet grows in the same scale event, the copied-forward `minCount` can exceed the shrunk PodSet's new `count`. 
The scheduler's `PodSetReducer` (`pkg/scheduler/flavorassigner/podset_reducer.go`) computes each PodSet's shrink budget as `count - minCount`; a `minCount` above `count` makes that budget negative, corrupting the shared total across all PodSets in the Workload.
Observed effect: the scale-up's replacement Workload never gets admitted, and the job is stuck pending even though quota is available for it.

**What you expected to happen**:
A PodSet whose `minCount` would exceed its own new `count` should have that floor capped at the new `count`, and the scale-up should still admit normally.

**How to reproduce it (as minimally and precisely as possible)**:

1. Enable `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlices` and `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlicesWithPartialReplicaScaleUp`.
2. Create a RayCluster with two worker groups, e.g. `workers-a` at 4 replicas and `workers-b` at 2 replicas, annotated for partial scale-up (`kueue.x-k8s.io/elastic-job-scale-up-strategy: partial`). Let it admit fully.
3. In one update, shrink `workers-a` to 1 replica while growing `workers-b` to 5 replicas.
4. Observe: no replacement Workload is ever admitted for the job; it stays stuck.

**Anything else we need to know?**:
We need to cap the copied minCount based on the minimum of its 'minCount` and `Count`.
Integration test will be provided for the fix.
