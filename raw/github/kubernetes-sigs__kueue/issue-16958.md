### Comment by [@ivnovakov](https://github.com/ivnovakov) — 2026-10-27T14:02:48Z

#17632 is merged, and the first Monday run had no timeouts.

I replayed the incidents since Aug 7 with the new dependabot days. The Friday incidents go away, and only Oct 8 (cherry-picks) and Oct 14 (`/ok-to-test` sweep) are left. Worst case of the 294 real timeouts that would still happen:

| Timeout | Still timing out |
|---|---|
| 14m | up to 135 (46%) |
| 16m | up to 79 (27%) |
| 20m | up to 19 (6%) |
| 22m | up to 7 (2%) |
| 25m | about 0 |

So 15 or 16m helps only a little. Oct 8 needed 20 to 26 min. A value above 15m works, since Prow counts the unscheduled timeout from Pod creation and the 15m pending timeout from Pod start.

We can also start fewer jobs per PR. A default PR starts 55 presubmits, and 41 of them are heavy. Two changes would cut that:
- Skip most jobs for bumps under kueueviz, `hack/debugpod` and `hack/releasing`. A kueueviz bump would go from 55 jobs to 3.
- Make the 1.35 and 1.36 e2e jobs optional on PRs and release branches. The 12h periodics already cover them.

Together that cuts the CPU of a cherry-pick PR by about a third, and cherry-picks are what hit us on Oct 8.

My proposal is to trim the jobs and raise the timeout to something like 20m, wdyt?
