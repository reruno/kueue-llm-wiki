## Description

### What happened?

Kueue releases all ClusterQueue quota when a managed RayJob's `JobDeploymentStatus` becomes `Complete` or `Failed`.

KubeRay can keep the RayCluster and all of its head and worker Pods running after that transition when `shutdownAfterJobFinishes: true` is combined with a nonzero `ttlSecondsAfterFinished`.

During that retention window, Kueue can admit another Workload using quota that is still physically occupied by the retained Ray Pods.

This behavior was initially discussed in #3994, but that issue was closed as not planned. The gap remains reproducible with Kueue v0.19.6 and KubeRay v1.7.0.

### Reproduction

Create a ClusterQueue with 500m CPU quota.

Submit a fixed-size RayJob requesting:

- head: 100m
- two workers: 100m each
- aggregate: 300m

Configure:

```yaml
shutdownAfterJobFinishes: true
ttlSecondsAfterFinished: 7200
```

After the RayJob reaches `SUCCEEDED`:

- its Workload has `Finished=True`;
- ClusterQueue CPU usage becomes zero;
- the RayCluster remains Ready;
- the head and both worker Pods remain Running and Ready;
- those Pods still request 300m CPU in total.

Submit another 300m Workload to the same ClusterQueue.

Observed:

- the second Workload is admitted;
- 600m of Kueue-admitted Pod requests remain running;
- the ClusterQueue reports only 300m usage against its 500m quota.

Whether the second Workload runs or remains Pending depends on physical node capacity. In either case, ClusterQueue quota has been assigned while the Pods that previously held it are still running.

### Expected behavior

Kueue should continue accounting for the completed RayJob's reservation while its RayCluster and Pods remain active during the configured retention period.

Quota should be released only after KubeRay has completed the full RayCluster cleanup.

### Proposed direction

Separate Ray application completion from Kueue Workload completion:

1. KubeRay may report that the Ray application has completed.
2. Kueue keeps the Workload admitted and retains its quota while the owned RayCluster or its Pods still exist.
3. KubeRay performs cleanup according to `shutdownAfterJobFinishes` and `ttlSecondsAfterFinished`.
4. After cleanup is confirmed, Kueue marks the Workload `Finished` and releases its quota.

This likely requires an explicit cleanup-complete signal or acknowledgment between the KubeRay and Kueue controllers. Ray application success alone is not sufficient because it precedes asynchronous RayCluster deletion.

### Out of scope

This issue covers the case where the full RayCluster, head and workers, is retained during `ttlSecondsAfterFinished`.

It does not cover deletion policies that remove workers while retaining the head Pod. That case may require partial accounting through Dynamic Reclaim and should be designed separately.
