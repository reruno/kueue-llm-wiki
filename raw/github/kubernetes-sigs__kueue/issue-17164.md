### Comment by [@dkaluza](https://github.com/dkaluza) — 2026-10-14T07:47:49Z

I'm not a DRA expert so please do not treat what I say as a general truth.

From my point of view it looks like you are trying to use consumable capacity:

https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/blob/d49db74ab4e060f0edd89fbccb898570e5417a46/keps/2941-DRA/README.md#consumable-capacity

To do that you should have appropriate ResourceSlices and Kueue configuration as in the example. Do you have those/can you share them?

I believe only "exactly" field is supported right now in Kueue for requesting those via  ResourceClaimTemplate which may be why your Workloads are not scheduled. I suspect that right now your requests may be interpreted as "give me one full gpu and additionally 7168 gpumem resource" - which might not be what you want.
