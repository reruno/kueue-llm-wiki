## Description

**What happened**:
When a `ClusterQueue` is configured with multiple TAS `ResourceFlavor`s (e.g., `flavor-a`, `flavor-b`) and `flavorFungibility.whenCanPreempt: TryNextFlavor`, Kueue can preempt a lower-priority workload on `flavor-a` instead of admitting the incoming workload on `flavor-b` without preemption.
Specifically, consider:
- `flavor-a` has enough free quota for the incoming workload, but its nodes/topology domains are occupied or fragmented by a lower-priority workload (fits by quota, but requires preemption in TAS).
- `flavor-b` has both free quota and free nodes (fits in both quota and TAS without preemption).

In this setup, Kueue selects `flavor-a` during quota evaluation, downgrades `flavor-a` from `Fit` to `Preempt` during TAS evaluation, finds the lower-priority victim on `flavor-a`, and immediately preempts it in the same scheduling cycle - never evaluating `flavor-b`.

**What you expected to happen**:
Since `flavorFungibility.whenCanPreempt: TryNextFlavor` is configured and `flavor-a` requires TAS preemption while `flavor-b` can admit the workload without preemption, Kueue should try `flavor-b` and admit the workload there without preempting the lower-priority workload on `flavor-a`.

**Anything else we need to know?**:
- Follow-up to #10824, #13691, #14008, and #16766 (see [discussion](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/13691#issuecomment-6078306373)).
- While #16766 tracks `TriedFlavors` as a set across scheduling cycles when a workload fails to admit, it does not prevent same-cycle preemption when `Fit` is downgraded to `Preempt` by TAS and victims are available.
- Potential direction discussed in:
  - https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/13691#issuecomment-6011106183
  - https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/13691#issuecomment-6078306373
