## Description

**What happened**:

Follow-up from #17194. ClusterQueues `a` and `b` (fair weight 1) share a Cohort with `c`, which lends 2 GPUs. `a` and `b` each run a 1-GPU workload borrowed from `c`, so both have a share of 500. Each also has a 2-CPU workload, and the Cohort has 2 CPUs, 1 nominal in `a` and 1 in `b`. Once `b`'s CPU workload runs, `a`'s preempts it, and preemptions continue as long as each eviction completes. Every preemption is reported as Fair Sharing within the cohort.

In envtest on main (7f64dc9ef), with each eviction finished within 200 ms, `a` preempted 26 times and `b` 83 times in 25 seconds. With `PrioritizePreemptorWorkloads` (alpha, off by default) it was 55 and 54 times.

**What you expected to happen**:

After at most one preemption, one of the two CPU workloads keeps running.

**How to reproduce it (as minimally and precisely as possible)**:

The spec below runs each case for 25 seconds and expects at most one preemption. On main, the two "tie at 500" entries fail. The two "nothing to lend for the GPU" entries withdraw the GPU quota of `a` and `b` instead of adding `c`. They pass on main and fail with #17194, where both shares become +Inf.

**Anything else we need to know?**:

The [no-loop proof](https://kueue.sigs.k8s.io/docs/concepts/fair_sharing/#proof-that-two-workloads-wont-preempt-each-other) assumes that admission raises a ClusterQueue's share and preemption lowers it, and notes that this can fail when the workloads don't use the dominant resource. Here `a` has a share of 500 with or without its CPU workload, and so does `b`, so `LessThanOrEqualToFinalShare` compares 500 with 500 and passes in both directions. #6774 found the same loop when preemption doesn't change a ClusterQueue's share, and #6925 fixed it for rounding and for zero weights; this case has neither. With #17194, a ClusterQueue that borrows a resource its Cohort can't lend has a share of +Inf, as agreed in #16529, so its other workloads can't change its share either.

Compared with `LessThanInitialShare`, [`LessThanOrEqualToFinalShare`](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/blob/7f64dc9ef2e476a6f7232af2e19d4a549c82f2fa/pkg/scheduler/preemption/fairsharing/strategy.go#L40-L43) adds only one kind of preemption: the one where the preemptor's new share and the target's share before and after preemption are all equal, as in this loop. Rejecting only that case makes all four entries of the spec pass with #17194 applied, and the fairsharing suite and the scheduler unit tests still pass:

```go
return schdcache.CompareDRS(schdcache.DRS(preemptorNewShare), schdcache.DRS(targetNewShare)) <= 0 &&
	schdcache.CompareDRS(schdcache.DRS(preemptorNewShare), schdcache.DRS(targetOldShare)) < 0
```

It changes [rule S2-a](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/blob/main/keps/1714-fair-sharing/README.md#choosing-workloads-from-clusterqueues-for-preemption) of the fair-sharing KEP, so it's worth agreeing on the approach here before the PR. cc @pajakd @JanKaczmarski
