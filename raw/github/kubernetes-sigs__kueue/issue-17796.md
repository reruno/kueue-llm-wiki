### Comment by [@thc1006](https://github.com/thc1006) — 2026-10-27T13:01:40Z

I think it's the `Store` in [`recordWorkloadAdmissionEvents`](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/blob/8d49f59e3bbe017aaff0896f0743bcef0dcecc9a/pkg/scheduler/scheduler.go#L1337-L1339). It runs after the admission status write returns, so when the Workload is deleted in between, the delete handler drops the entry and this call stores it again. The [failed run](https://storage.googleapis.com/kubernetes-ci-logs/pr-logs/pull/kubernetes-sigs_kueue/17793/pull-kueue-test-integration-shard-2-release-0-20/2115034721447579648/build-log.txt) has that order: the delete event for `wl-removed` at 11:00:09.268, then "Workload successfully admitted" at 11:00:09.286.

#14577 replaces that `Store` with `MakeValsSet`, as @Singularity23x0 [suggested there](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/14577#issuecomment-5539950037). I reproduced the leak with a unit test that deletes the Workload from the cache right after the status write: the entry stays on main and release-0.20, and is gone with that change. For release-0.20 the LocalQueue part is enough, since the ClusterQueue part is the #13589 feature.
