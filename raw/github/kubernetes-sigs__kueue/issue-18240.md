**What happened**:

When a ClusterQueue uses a ProvisioningRequest admission check, a workload whose containers use a block-mode volumeClaimTemplate through `volumeDevices` (StatefulSet, LeaderWorkerSet) gets no ProvisioningRequest. The PodTemplate built from the PodSet is rejected, because the device's volume is not present in the template:

```
PodTemplate "..." is invalid: template.spec.containers[0].volumeDevices[0].name: Not found: "state"
```

**What you expected to happen**:

The ProvisioningRequest is created. Devices that are not backed by a PersistentVolumeClaim or Ephemeral volume should be left out of the PodTemplate copy only, without changing the Workload.

**How to reproduce it (as minimally and precisely as possible)**:

Create a StatefulSet with a block-mode volumeClaimTemplate used as a `volumeDevice`, queued to a ClusterQueue with a ProvisioningRequest admission check. The workload stays pending and the error above is logged.

**Anything else we need to know?**:

This is the `volumeDevices` counterpart of the missing-volume `volumeMount` case.
