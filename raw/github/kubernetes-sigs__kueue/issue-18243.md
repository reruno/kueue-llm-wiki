## Description

<!-- Please use this template while reporting a bug and provide as much info as possible. Not doing so may result in your bug not being addressed in a timely manner. Thanks! -->

**What happened**:

KubeRay injects an autoscaler sidecar (in-tree autoscaling) and, for RayJob `submissionMode: SidecarMode`, a submitter container into the head Pod. Kueue mirrors these containers on the head PodSet for quota accounting, but without an image. With a ProvisioningRequest admission check, the PodTemplate is then rejected with `template.spec.containers[N].image: Required value`, and the Workload is stuck.

**What you expected to happen**:

The ProvisioningRequest is created. Its PodTemplate fills an empty container image from another container in the same PodSet (for Ray, the head Ray container image), without changing the Workload. Workloads already stuck on this error, including elastic slices, recover without being recreated. This applies to RayJob, RayCluster and RayService.

**How to reproduce it (as minimally and precisely as possible)**:

Submit a RayCluster with in-tree autoscaling, or a RayJob in SidecarMode, to a ClusterQueue with a ProvisioningRequest admission check. No ProvisioningRequest is created.
