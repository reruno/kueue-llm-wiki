**What happened**:

For an elastic SparkApplication, a failed executor (for example, an OOM kill, an eviction or a bad node) during the initial ramp-up ends the initial-executor hold. The admitted slice then shrinks while the driver still wants its initial executors.

With `initialExecutors: 4`: the driver created exec-1..3 and exec-3 failed. `countExecutorPods` counts only Pending and Running pods (`count` = 2) but counts every pod's ID in `maxID` (`created` = 3). Because `created <= count` is false, `numExecutors` returns 2 and the slice shrinks from 4 to 2. The driver then creates exec-4 and a replacement exec-5. Both stay gated until a new scale-up slice is admitted, and on a busy ClusterQueue the freed quota can go to another workload first.

The recorded `spark-max-executor-id` annotation keeps the hold off for the rest of the submission after the failed pod is deleted. The replacement executor (ID 4) also makes `createdAll` true while the driver has only 3 executors.

**What you expected to happen**:

Kueue keeps the initial reservation while Spark replaces failed executors. A real scale-down (the driver stopping or deleting executors) still releases quota.

**How to reproduce it (as minimally and precisely as possible)**:

Add a `TestPodSetsExecutorCount` case with `initialExecutors: 4`, exec-1 and exec-2 `Running`, and exec-3 `Failed`. It fails with `Count = 2, want 4`. Repeat it after the failed pod is deleted, with the max executor ID recorded in the annotation.

**Anything else we need to know?**:

Code: `numExecutors` and `countExecutorPods` in `pkg/controller/jobs/sparkapplication/sparkapplication_controller.go`.

Possible fix from the review: do not count failed executors as a stop. Skipping Failed pods in `maxID` covers the time before the pod is deleted. Covering the time after it needs the failed executor IDs (or their number) recorded per submission next to the max ID, and subtracted from `created`. In spark-operator v2.5.2 a clean exit becomes `COMPLETED` and a pod the driver deletes becomes `UNKNOWN`, so the existing "stopped" and "deleted" cases still end the hold.
