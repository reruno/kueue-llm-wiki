## Description

/kind bug

**What happened**

In a ClusterQueue that references a `PreemptionConfig`, a pending workload whose rules give it no preemption targets stays at the head of the queue. It blocks a later, equal-priority workload that the rules *do* allow to preempt. The blocked workload never gets a `QuotaReserved` condition while the head is pending. We saw this for 420s+ in a minimal reproducer and 898s+ in a larger MultiKueue setup. It doesn't clear on its own.

**What you expected**

A head with no eligible victims is marked inadmissible (`PreemptionNoCandidates`), as with classical preemption. Under `BestEffortFIFO` the next workload is then evaluated and preempts.

**How to reproduce** (single cluster, no MultiKueue)

Feature gates `ConfigurablePreemptions=true`, alpha CRDs enabled. One ClusterQueue with 8 units of an extended resource:

```yaml
apiVersion: kueue.x-k8s.io/v1alpha1
kind: PreemptionConfig
metadata: {name: ladder}
spec:
  rules:
  - name: prod-evicts-dev-except-team-b
    preemptorSelector:
      matchExpressions:
      - {key: example.com/class, operator: In, values: [prod]}
      - {key: example.com/team, operator: NotIn, values: [b]}
    activationPolicy: {trigger: Always}
    candidateSelectors:
    - scope: WithinClusterQueue
      labelSelector: {matchLabels: {example.com/class: dev}}
      priority: {mode: Base, comparison: LessThan}
---
apiVersion: kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta2
kind: ClusterQueue
metadata:
  name: cq
  annotations: {kueue.x-k8s.io/preemption-config-name: ladder}
spec:
  namespaceSelector: {}
  preemption: {withinClusterQueue: Never, reclaimWithinCohort: Never}
  resourceGroups:
  - coveredResources: [cpu, example.com/gpu]
    flavors:
    - name: default
      resources: [{name: cpu, nominalQuota: "100"}, {name: example.com/gpu, nominalQuota: "8"}]
```

WorkloadPriorityClasses `prod` (30000) and `dev` (10000). `integrations.labelKeysToCopy: [example.com/class, example.com/team]`. One LocalQueue `lq` per namespace (`team-a`, `team-b`).

1. Fill the queue: 8 × 1-unit Jobs in `team-a`, `priority-class: dev`, `example.com/class: dev`.
2. Submit 1 Job in `team-b` with `priority-class: prod`, `example.com/class: prod`, `example.com/team: b`. It is excluded by `preemptorSelector`, so it correctly stays pending.
3. Submit 2 Jobs in `team-a` with `priority-class: prod`, `example.com/class: prod`, `example.com/team: a`. They are allowed by the rule.

| Pending excluded heads (step 2) | team-a prod admitted |
|---|---|
| 0 | after 7s ✅ |
| 1 | not after 420s ❌ |
| 2 | not after 420s ❌ |

**Anything else**

- `pkg/scheduler/flavorassigner/flavorassigner.go`, `usesConfigurablePreemption()`, widens the preemption check for any ClusterQueue with a `PreemptionConfig`. Its comment says whether a rule is actually triggered "is determined by the preemption algorithm itself". So the flavor assigner returns `Preempt` mode even when no rule can fire for this preemptor.
- In the larger setup (MultiKueue worker), the blocked workload had no `QuotaReserved` condition at all, and `status.preemptionGates` were empty. The result was the same with `MultiKueueOrchestratedPreemption` on or off, so the preemption gate isn't involved.
- Related, but these don't look like the same fix: #18328 (BestEffortFIFO starvation after skipped attempts) covers `skipped`, not this path. #18271 (timed requeue when no candidates match yet) covers time filters.
- Possible fix: in the flavor assigner, evaluate the `PreemptionConfig` rules for the preemptor (`preemptorSelector` + candidate selectors) when deciding `Preempt` vs `NoFit`, so a preemptor no rule applies to gets `NoFit`/inadmissible and the head moves on.

**Environment**

- Kueue v0.20.5 (chart and image), and main @ `fd28736de`: same result (0 heads: admitted after 4s; 1 or 2 heads: not after 420s). On main, a dump of the Workloads at the end of the window shows only the excluded head has `QuotaReserved=False/WaitingForQuota`; the allowed `team-a` workloads have no `QuotaReserved` condition at all, i.e. they are never evaluated.
- Kubernetes v1.34.0 (kubeadm, single node)
- Classic preemption off (`withinClusterQueue: Never`, `reclaimWithinCohort: Never`), as the configurable-preemption docs describe for rules-only queues

## Discussion

### Comment by [@NasitSony](https://github.com/NasitSony) — 2026-11-07T22:50:12Z

Traced this and I think the diagnosis points at the wrong place.

`usesConfigurablePreemption()` is only used in `canPreemptWhileBorrowing()`, and in your config it isn't reached — nominal quota is 8 and the request fits within it, so line 1151's first condition passes and `SimulatePreemption` runs unconditionally.

The preemption path looks correct for your case. `candidatesFor` checks `matchesPreemptor(&rule, p.preemptor)` and skips non-matching rules, so the excluded team-b preemptor should collect no candidates, and the oracle returns `NoCandidates` when `len(candidates) == 0`. That should give the head `noFit`.

Your observation that the blocked team-a workloads have no `QuotaReserved` condition at all is the more telling detail — it suggests they're never evaluated rather than evaluated and rejected. That would point at the queue rather than the flavor assigner: a head that gets `noFit` should be marked inadmissible and the next entry considered.

Haven't traced that side yet. Worth checking whether the head is actually getting `noFit` — the `inadmissibleMsg` on it would say.
