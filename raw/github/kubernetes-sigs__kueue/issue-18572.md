## Description



**What happened**:

Netlify didn't generate preview for the website change here: https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/18552.

At the same time the CI verification job 
pull-kueue-verify-website-links-preview-main was triggered and failing on missing preview

**What you expected to happen**:

The preview is generated whenever the CI verification job is running. It may be generated on a superset of PRs, but not a subset of PRs that the CI verification job is running.
