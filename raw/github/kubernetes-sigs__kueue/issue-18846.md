Migrate the 3 legacy flvResQ cases in TestDominantResourceShare (pkg/cache/scheduler/fair_sharing_test.go) to record workload usage directly lets us drop wlReq and simplify dominantResourceShare to a single loop over rn.Usage (usage.Sub(rn.SubtreeQuota[fr]) > 0).
