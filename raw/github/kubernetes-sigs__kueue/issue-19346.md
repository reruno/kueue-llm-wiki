I’ve reviewed the history of our CI jobs and found that while the actual test execution time is quite short, the processes for building images and preparing the environment take a significant amount of time. I plan to parallelize these tasks—specifically the build/compilation phase and the cluster environment setup.

I will also look into configuration adjustments to reduce CPU contention and minimize wait times.

My CI agents are already up and running. Once these two areas are optimized, I’ll re-evaluate the duration and determine the next steps for further improvement. The goal is to bring the total time under 10 minutes—all while ensuring that quality remains uncompromised.
