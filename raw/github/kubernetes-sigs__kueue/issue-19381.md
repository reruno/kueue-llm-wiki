**What would you like to be documented or improved**:

Make the aggregated unit coverage discoverable from the repository README, as suggested in https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/18136#issuecomment-7095649802.

Since #18136, main's merged unit coverage profile from `post-kueue-test-unit-coverage-main` is uploaded to Codecov. The reports are live, but nothing in the repository links to them.

Proposal:

- Add a Codecov badge at the top of `README.md`, linked to the Codecov dashboard:
  - badge: https://codecov.io/gh/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/branch/main/graph/badge.svg
  - dashboard: https://app.codecov.io/gh/kubernetes-sigs/kueue
- Add a short "Test coverage" note in `README.md` linking:
  - the Codecov dashboard;
  - the Prow job history: https://prow.k8s.io/job-history/gs/kubernetes-ci-logs/logs/post-kueue-test-unit-coverage-main
  - the TestGrid tab: https://testgrid.k8s.io/sig-scheduling-kueue#post-kueue-test-unit-coverage-main
