#### Special notes for your reviewer:

By passing `localClient` to `DeleteRemoteObject`, we can list local pods in the group (via the field index) and reuse their Name/Namespace to delete the corresponding pods with `remoteClient`.

Due to change to signature of `DeleteRemoteObject` in `MultiKueueAdapter` interface, updated all implementing adapters.
