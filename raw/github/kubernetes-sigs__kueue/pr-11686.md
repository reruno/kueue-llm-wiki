  This introduces a strict dependency on the controller populating `p.list.Items` with at least the expected group size. If the intended contract is 'exactly tc pods must be present and ready', consider enforcing `len(p.list.Items) == tc` explicitly; if the contract is 'at least tc ready pods', consider counting ready pods and comparing to `tc` instead of requiring every listed pod to be ready.
