#### What this PR does / why we need it:

Without pre-pulling and kind-loading the operator and config-reloader images, kubelet had to pull them from quay.io inside the 5m kubectl wait budget and without retries, causing a flaky timeout on deployments/prometheus-operator.

Added pre-pull of both images in prepare_docker_images and kind_load, before applying the bundle in install_prometheus_operator.
