  AFAIU this test could pass even without your fix.
  Because the order of LocalQueues returned from client.List is non-deterministic.
  So, even without your fix, it could happen that lq2 is returned first, lq1 later, then lq1 overrides lq2 as the value under `lq.Name`... and everything works, from the perspective of pod1.
  (In this scenario, it would fail if we had a pod2 in ns2, purposed for cq2 via lq2).
  
  Hence, I'd suggest to:
  - define pod2 in this test as well
  - pass both namespaces to `Load`
  - verify that pod1 is admitted in cq1 _and_ pod2 is admitted in cq2
  
  And this will make a test case that would fail without your fix in 100% of cases.
  
  WDYT?
