## Walkthrough

`handleElasticWorkload` and `handleScaleDown` in the TAS elastic workloads scheduler are updated to propagate the leader pod set's previous topology assignment into `result` and `assumedUsage` for both the scale-down and same-count cases. A new `finalizeElasticAssignment` helper consolidates the result-building logic. Two new test cases validate leader reuse under the relevant feature gates.

## Changes

**TAS elastic workload leader propagation fix**

| Layer / File(s) | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Leader propagation in `handleElasticWorkload`, `handleScaleDown`, and `finalizeElasticAssignment`** <br> `pkg/cache/scheduler/tas_elastic_workloads.go` | `handleElasticWorkload` passes `leader` to `handleScaleDown` on the scale-down path and delegates the same-count result-building to a new `finalizeElasticAssignment` helper. `handleScaleDown` gains a `leader *TASPodSetRequests` parameter, truncates the workers' previous assignment, then calls `finalizeElasticAssignment`. The new `finalizeElasticAssignment` helper builds result from the workers' (possibly truncated) assignment and conditionally appends the leader's previous assignment and its resource usage to `assumedUsage`. |
| **Test cases for leader reuse** <br> `pkg/cache/scheduler/tas_cache_test.go` | Two new entries in `TestFindTopologyAssignments` cover scale-down (workers truncated, leader reused unchanged) and same-count (both leader and workers reused exactly) elastic scenarios with leader in the same `podSetGroupName`, guarded by `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlices` and `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlicesWithTAS` feature gates. |
