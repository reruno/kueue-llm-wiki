## Description

#### What type of PR is this?
/kind bug

#### What this PR does / why we need it?

Fair sharing already lets a ClusterQueue that stays within its own nominal quota preempt regardless of DominantResourceShare (DRS). That entitlement stopped at the ClusterQueue level, so in hierarchical setups a Cohort's own nominal quota gave its descendants no such preference: a workload could be blocked from reclaiming quota belonging to its parent Cohort purely because the Cohort's aggregate DRS was inflated by borrowing on an unrelated flavor.

This PR evaluates the nominal claim **per preemption candidate**, at the preemptor's *almostLCA* with that candidate's target. The claim holds when that node is within nominal quota for every flavor-resource the incoming workload requests. The same check replaces the ClusterQueue-level one in both candidate selection and the final-target verification (`FairSharingVerifyFinalTargets`).

Fixes #9466

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

**AI usage disclosure:** per the [Kubernetes AI Tool Usage Policy](https://www.kubernetes.dev/docs/guide/pull-requests/#ai-guidance), this PR was developed with AI assistance (Claude Code) for analysis, implementation, and test authoring.

1. **The claim is checked only at the almostLCA.** That is the level at which the scheduling tournament compares the preemptor's and target's subtrees (`computeDRS` keys a cohort's tournament by its children). A claim granted at any other level can be undone on the next cycle: the tournament re-admits the evicted workload ahead of the preemptor, and preemption repeats. The new unit case "no claim when the AlmostLCA borrows, even if a lower cohort is within nominal" covers this loop.

2. **Behavior change for the ClusterQueue-level claim.** A ClusterQueue within its own nominal quota no longer has a claim against a target whose almostLCA with it is a borrowing Cohort. This also closes the same loop on main when the ClusterQueue, rather than an intermediate Cohort, holds the quota. `TestScheduleRecomputePreemptionTargets` "hierarchical nominal-first prefers non-borrowing leaf CQ" is updated accordingly: the final preemptions are unchanged, but `wl-hero` now targets `wl-noisy-admitted` directly instead of first targeting `wl-rest-admitted` in another cohort and recomputing. Recompute under fair sharing remains covered by the next case in that test.

3. **Requested resources, not only contested ones.** Following #17034, the check covers every flavor-resource the incoming workload requests.

The behavior is gated by `FairSharingPreemptWithinNominal` (Beta, on by default since 0.17).

#### Release note

```release-note
FairSharing: Fixed a bug where a workload could be blocked from reclaiming its parent Cohort's nominal quota when that Cohort's DominantResourceShare was inflated by borrowing on an unrelated flavor. The nominal-quota preemption claim is now evaluated at the level where the preemptor's and target's subtrees meet, so descendants of a Cohort get preferential access to the Cohort's own nominal quota, and a ClusterQueue within its own nominal quota no longer preempts across a borrowing parent Cohort.
```
