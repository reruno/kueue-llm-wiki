## Description

#### What type of PR is this?

/kind bug

#### What this PR does / why we need it:

When a workload requires preemption/reclamation of multiple target workloads across a cohort to acquire their combined quota, desynchronized eviction completion times can cause preemption thrashing and infinite preemption loops.

Specifically, if one target workload (`wlA`) finishes evicting and releases its quota early, the preempting workload (`preemptor-wl`) cannot yet be admitted because it is still waiting for remaining target workloads (`wlB`) to finish evicting. During this window, because the pending quota for `preemptor-wl` is not reserved or locked in the persistent cache across scheduling cycles:

1. Target workloads (or other workloads) can immediately reclaim or steal the newly freed quota if their queue has sufficient nominal capacity or under fair sharing allocation.
2. When `wlA` is re-admitted, `preemptor-wl` remains blocked and must re-issue a preemption request against `wlA`.
3. When `wlB` subsequently completes eviction, it similarly steals the freed quota and gets re-admitted, causing preemptor-wl to issue another preemption against `wlB`.
4. If eviction completions are desynchronized, target workloads alternate between evicted and re-admitted states, creating a preemption thrashing loop that repeatedly evicts workloads without ever allowing `preemptor-wl` to acquire the combined quota it needs.

To resolve this, this PR introduces a prioritization mechanism guarded by the `PrioritizePreemptorWorkloads` feature gate that leverages ClusterQueue preemptor tracking (`preemptorWorkload`):

* **Preemptor Tracking at Queue Head:** When preemption is initiated, the ClusterQueue records the active preemptor workload (`preemptorWorkload`) and its evaluated `.metadata.generation`. When popping queue heads in `manager.Heads()`, we query `cq.IsPreemptor(wl)` (verifying strict generation matching) and stamp `IsPreemptor: true` directly onto the scheduler's `Head` struct.
* **Iterator Prioritization:** Under the `PrioritizePreemptorWorkloads` feature gate, scheduler iterators (`makeClassicalIterator` in Classical and `entryComparer.less` in Fair Sharing) prioritize candidate workloads with `IsPreemptor == true` at the top of the evaluation order.
* **Snapshot Quota Reservation:** When evaluated first, active preemptors reserve their required capacity in the scheduling cycle's snapshot (`cq.AddUsage`), even when `ReclaimWithinCohort: Any` (`CanAlwaysReclaim`) is configured. This effectively locks the quota freed during desynchronized target evictions and prevents lower-priority or competitor workloads from stealing it mid-eviction.
