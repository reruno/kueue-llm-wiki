#### What this PR does / why we need it?

Follow-up to #16074. Replaces the local `makeWl` helpers in `TestUpdateSettlesAfsEntryPenaltyPerReservation` and `TestUpdateDropsUnsettleableAfsEntryPenalty` with a `baseWl` wrapper and derives each fixture with `Clone()`, matching the pattern used in `TestUpdateSettlesAfsEntryPenalty`. Test-only change; the fixtures and test cases are unchanged.

Part of #16030

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

- With this PR, no `makeWl` helpers remain in `workload_controller_test.go`.
- AI disclosure: I used an AI assistant (Claude) to help clarify the intended replacement. I made the change myself, reviewed the diff, and ran the unit tests, `make ci-lint` and `make verify` locally.
