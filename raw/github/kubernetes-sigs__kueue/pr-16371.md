#### What this PR does / why we need it?

For example, a RayCluster has an admitted Workload slice with 1 head Pod, 4 workers-a Pods, and 2 workers-b Pods, filling its quota of 7. 

In one update, workers-a shrinks to 1 replica while workers-b grows to 6. The new target is 8 Pods, but partial replica scale-up should admit 7 :  ` 1 head, 1 workers-a, and 5 workers-b `.

The replacement Workload correctly caps workers-a’s MinCount at its new target of 1.  During scheduling, however, Kueue overwrites that minimum with the old slice’s grant of 4. This makes the minimum larger than the target and gives the reducer a negative shrink budget. The replacement stays pending even though a valid 7-Pod allocation fits the quota. 

The correct way is When using the predecessor’s granted count as a replacement PodSet’s scheduling minimum, cap it at the PodSet’s new target: `MinCount = min(old granted count, new Count)`
