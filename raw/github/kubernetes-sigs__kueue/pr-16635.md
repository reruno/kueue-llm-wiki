#### What type of PR is this?

/kind kep

#### What this PR does / why we need it?

Adds KEP-16471, proposing `integrations.annotationKeysToCopy`: a Configuration field, analogous to `labelKeysToCopy` (KEP-1834), that copies the listed annotations from the job object into the Workload at creation time for every integration, independent of the `CustomMetricLabels` feature gate. Targeted at v0.21.

Part of #16471

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

- Deliberate differences from `labelKeysToCopy`: keys are validated at configuration load, and keys under `kueue.x-k8s.io/` are rejected.
- No feature gate is proposed; the reasoning is the first entry in Alternatives.
- `kep.yaml` metadata follows KEP-1834 and KEP-7066 and is open to adjustment.
- Context: #16187 (fix for #13277).
- AI assistance (Claude Code) was used to draft this KEP; I reviewed all of it.
