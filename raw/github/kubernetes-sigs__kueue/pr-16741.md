#### What this PR does / why we need it?

Adds the alpha, disabled-by-default `MultiKueueClusterNames` feature gate and the
`kueue.x-k8s.io/multikueue-cluster-names` annotation. When enabled, a Job's
annotation is propagated to its Workload, and MultiKueue restricts dispatch to
the intersection of the requested worker clusters and the clusters authorized
by its `MultiKueueConfig`. Requested names are sorted and deduplicated; a
request with no authorized clusters remains unscheduled.

This supports callers that already know where their data or checkpoints reside
and need to dispatch a job to that worker cluster.

Related: #14539 and #14540.
