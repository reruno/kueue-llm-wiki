**Security Blast Radius**
- _inferred_ — The relevant failure-containment scope is workload status and the scheduling-cycle snapshot shared across queues, including simulated victim usage and pods. Current planner return paths do not expose the new assignment-error outcome to workload authors.

**Trust Boundaries and Controls**
- _observed_ — Error handling retains the workload identity. The existing requeue operation holds its manager lock, rereads the namespaced workload, derives its current LocalQueue, and avoids duplicate requeue insertion rather than relying solely on stale scheduler queue membership.

**Resilience and Maintainability Implications**
- _observed_ — Status patching follows requeue and requests conflict retries; a reported patch failure is logged. Requeue releases inflight ownership before its client read, so cancellation recovery is not established by this path. That ordering and completion behavior are unchanged from the PR base and are not retained as introduced concerns.

**Hardening Proposals**
- _proposed_ — Before enabling an error-producing planner, establish its failure contract: validity of returned results, propagation of partial-admission errors, restoration of planner-owned mutations, and suitability of diagnostic text for workload-visible status.
