## AI summary

When `UnadmittedWorkloadsObservability` is enabled, the scheduler now resolves the assignment’s NoFit reason after planning. This applies the resolution regardless of which planner produced the assignment. The native planner no longer resolves the reason itself.

### Suggested release note

**Observability:** Fixed NoFit reasons not being resolved for assignments produced by planners other than the native planner. When `UnadmittedWorkloadsObservability` is enabled, Kueue now resolves the reason after planning.
