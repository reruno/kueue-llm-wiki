#### What this PR does / why we need it:
I believe I've met the requirements and would like to self-nominate as a Kueue Reviewer. I'm already a reviewer for `kueue-populator` (#12431) and would like to extend that to the whole codebase.
- [x] Member for at least 3 months: kubernetes/org#6187 (since March 2026)
- [x] Primary reviewer for at least 5 PRs to the codebase: [16 merged PRs](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+assignee%3Aj-skiba+-author%3Aj-skiba). Some of them:
    - #12344 - `TASReplaceMultipleFailedNodes`: incremental node replacement without eviction (KEP; 13 review comments, 5 applied as suggestions)
    - #15640 - TAS: place a partial PodSet slice
    - #13819 - refactor: Reuse TAS topology trees across scheduler snapshots
- [x] Reviewed or merged at least 20 substantial PRs to the codebase:
  - [Reviewed and commented on 43 PRs](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pulls?q=is%3Apr+reviewed-by%3Aj-skiba+commenter%3Aj-skiba+-author%3Aj-skiba)
  - [Merged 131 PRs](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Aj-skiba+is%3Amerged) total, 85 of them original (the rest are release-branch cherry-picks):

My contributions cluster around four areas of Kueue:
- **Topology-Aware Scheduling: node failure handling.** I authored the KEP and implementation for node replacement on tainted nodes (#8929, #8964, #8997), fixed stuck pods during node replacement (#9615) and NodeHotSwap performance issues (#10042), and added preferred node affinity support (#10903).
- **Observability of inadmissible workloads.** I authored the KEP (#11720, follow-ups #12812, #12814) and its implementation: flavor assigner diagnostic reasons (#12378), scheduler reasons (#12452), workload controller status reasons (#12510) and unadmitted workloads metrics (#12759).
- **Scheduler internals & quota.** I abstracted resource requests behind the `Requests` interface (#13252, #13300, #13338), worked on flavor scan state (#16766, #17069), co-authored the Dynamic Quota Orchestration KEP (#14104) and implemented EffectiveQuota (#14389, #15040, #15107, #15199). I also authored the WaitForPodsReady Maximum Not-Ready Pods KEP (#16188, #16059).
- **kueue-populator.** I authored the KEP (#7611) and initial implementation (#7655, #7814), and I'm currently its reviewer.
