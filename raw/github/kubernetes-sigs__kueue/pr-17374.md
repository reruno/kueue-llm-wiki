#### What this PR does / why we need it?

An elastic TAS Job can keep its old topology reservation after shrinking. For example, four Pods fill two nodes; after the Job shrinks to two Pods on one node, a waiting Job still cannot use the empty node.

Once the removed Pods have terminated, reduce the topology assignment to the domains occupied by the surviving bound Pods. Requeue waiting Workloads when that reservation shrinks. Keep the existing reservation while the Pod observation is incomplete or a survivor's placement cannot be validated. If a survivor's Node is missing, continue ungating other PodSets and retry shrinking the reservation.

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

Unit coverage verifies that a missing survivor Node preserves the entire admission, allows another PodSet to ungate, and returns a retry error. A subsequent reconcile after only the Node becomes available releases the unused reservation.

A direct queue-update regression isolates the shrink-triggered wakeup and fails when that notification is disabled. Integration tests demonstrate capacity reuse after termination, grouped-slice preservation, and regrowth from zero; they can receive other wakeup signals. These tests use envtest Kubernetes 1.37.0 with simulated Pod creation, binding, and termination.

Validation:

- `go test ./pkg/controller/tas ./pkg/controller/core ./pkg/cache/queue -count=1`
- Job integration group: `Job controller with TAS and ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlices`
- `make verify`

AI assistance was used to prepare the patch and tests.
