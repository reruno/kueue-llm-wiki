## AI summary

When DRA charges are refreshed while a workload is checked out, Kueue now preserves them on requeue and avoids leaving the workload inadmissible based on its old charges.

### Suggested release note

DRA: Fixed a bug where requeueing a workload after its DRA charges were refreshed could restore stale charges and delay scheduling. Kueue now requeues the workload with the refreshed charges. This behavior is gated by the `KueueDRAIntegration` feature gate.

  When a DRA refresh is detected during checkout, `requeueWorkloadLocked` uses `RequeueIfNotPresentImmediately` for every reason. For a StrictFIFO ClusterQueue, a workload returned with `RequeueReasonNamespaceMismatch` then enters the active heap, not the inadmissible set.
