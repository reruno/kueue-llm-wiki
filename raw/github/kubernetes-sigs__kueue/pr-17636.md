### Comment by [@sohankunkerkar](https://github.com/sohankunkerkar) — 2026-11-02T14:49:32Z

> @sohankunkerkar AFAIK, the current Kueue DRA CC already supports `allocationMode: All`, right?

Not yet. All is rejected for every DRA request, CC included. The device count depends on the node, and Kueue charges quota before it knows one. It's already a GA item in the KEP, since it needs the charge moved after placement, likely through scheduler-library. The more urgent case is #18171, filed today: unmapped companion devices like RDMA NICs use All and block admission even though there's nothing to charge. I think we should fix that one first.
