## Description

#### What type of PR is this?

/kind cleanup

/area tas

#### What this PR does / why we need it?

Moves `TASAssignmentsResult` and the definitions it depends on out of `pkg/cache/scheduler/tas_flavor_snapshot.go` into a new file, `tas_assignments_result.go`, to reduce the size of `tas_flavor_snapshot.go` and improve readability.

Moved:
- `TASAssignmentsResult`, with its `Failure()` and `Failures()` methods
- `FailureInfo` (returned by `Failure()`/`Failures()`)
- `tasPodSetAssignmentResult` (the map's value type)

`TestTASAssignmentsResultFailure` is moved accordingly, from `tas_flavor_snapshot_test.go` into `tas_assignments_result_test.go`.

Fixes #17303

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

- `TASPodSetRequests` and `FlavorTASRequests` are intentionally left in `tas_flavor_snapshot.go`, since they don't depend on `TASAssignmentsResult` and the issue scopes the change to it.
