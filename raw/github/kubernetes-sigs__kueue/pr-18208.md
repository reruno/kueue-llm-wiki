#### What this PR does / why we need it:

Refreshes the Kueue website's Talks, Case Studies & Tutorials page (`/community/talks_and_presentations/`) and Community page banners (`/community/` & `/community/showcase/`):

- **Talks, Case Studies & Tutorials (`/community/talks_and_presentations/`):**
  - Expand the page title from *Talks & Case Studies* to *Talks, Case Studies & Tutorials* and display publication dates with month granularity (`Jan 2006`).
  - Add 12 recent talks, adopter case studies, and technical tutorials (including the 4-part *DRA Explained for Kueue Users* series, Headlamp tutorial, Cohere, AI21 Labs, Ant Group, China Merchants Bank, Google Cloud Console monitoring, MultiKueue GPU Hunter, and DaoCloud TAS), fix Runway's case study URL, and remove Meta's broken schedule link.
  - Support multiple tags per entry (`type: ["talk", "case-study"]` or `["talk", "tutorial"]`) and `?q=` query pre-filtering.
- **Community & Adopters/Resources Pages (`/community/` & `/community/showcase/`):**
  - Render each page's own title and description in `layouts/community/list.html` to eliminate duplicate headers, rename the *User Showcase* tab to *Adopters & Resources* (*Adopters, Talks & Resources*), and update the Medium feed link to *View all Kueue stories on Medium*.
