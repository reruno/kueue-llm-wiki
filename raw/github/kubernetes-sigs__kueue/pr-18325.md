#### What this PR does / why we need it?

The metrics of a deleted LocalQueue can come back after the deletion and stay. This affects the counters and histograms recorded for a Workload, such as `kueue_local_queue_evicted_workloads_total`.

Most of them are checked against the scheduler cache, which releases its lock before they are recorded. Nine call sites check before the status write in `Evict` and record after it. A record that lands after the clear brings the series back.

The call sites that checked the scheduler cache now record through `Cache.RecordLocalQueueMetricsForWorkload`, which checks the LocalQueue and `localQueueSelector` and records under one read lock. Deleting the LocalQueue clears its metrics under the cache write lock, so a record either finishes before the clear or finds no LocalQueue. `Evict` makes this call after its status write, with the Workload the write returns. `kueue_local_queue_workload_creation_latency_seconds` keeps its queue manager check, which now records under the queue manager's read lock. The delete handler removes the LocalQueue from the queue manager before the cache clears the metrics.

The `kueue_local_queue_unadmitted_workloads` gauge was fixed separately in #18608.

Fixes #18322

The cache, `Evict` and queue manager tests use the new signatures, so they do not build on `main`. I removed each new check in the cache and in `Evict` in turn, and a named test failed each time. The one ordering without a test is the clear under the cache write lock, which only a forced interleaving would show.

This PR moves the `metrics.ClearLocalQueueMetrics` call that #17968 added after `cache.DeleteLocalQueue` into it, so the delete and the clear happen under one write lock. The delete holds that lock while the series are removed.

Not changed here, and older than this PR:
- The LocalQueue controller's own reports of the LocalQueue status and the Admission Fair Sharing usage. A reconcile that read the LocalQueue before the deletion can still report them after the clear. I would fix them after #17457, which changes the LocalQueue gauge resync.
- Clearing the metrics of a LocalQueue that the selector stops matching, or whose custom labels change. A record that checked the LocalQueue before the change can still write after the clear.
- A LocalQueue that is deleted and re-created while the informer misses the deletion arrives as an update with a new UID and keeps its series.
