Some representative primary reviews:

- _Scheduler, queueing and preemption correctness:_
  - #16339 (XXL): reproduced cascading preemption when a victim's quota was released before its Pods terminated, evicting unrelated workloads.
  - #15828 (XXL, in-flight): found recovery gaps that stranded Workloads after late LocalQueue creation or RuntimeClass recovery, plus a regression blocking TAS second pass.
  - #15761 (XXL): caught an event ordering that could leave a stale speculative assignment in the cache and double-book a node; suggested the fix that was adopted.
  - #13819 (XXL): caught a domain-ID collision between leaf and ancestor domains in reused TAS topology trees.
- _Fair sharing / AFS:_
  - #13570 (XL): requested regression coverage for LocalQueue weight changes with queued workloads, and proposed sharing the weight lookup between the heap and the snapshot (later #16241).
  - #15384 (L, in-flight): reproduced two held-LocalQueue regressions: stale AFS metrics after deletion, and repeated reconcile failures while the ClusterQueue was missing.
- _Pod integration and upgrade safety:_
  - #14809 (XL): identified PodSet-order drift between reconstructed PodSets and existing Workloads, including across feature-gate rollout and rollback, that could disrupt running groups.

I also helped break down larger changes and coordinate overlapping work:

- #14078 (delayed TAS second pass): identified the queue, admission-write, and scheduler-cache boundaries; proposed staged fixes (#16462, #16468) while keeping the admission-write step in #15761.
- #16339 / #16355 (residual TAS Pods): flagged the overlapping fixes, reviewed the extracted work (#17460, #17809), and explored a smaller first step for the rest.

My authored work focuses on scheduler and fair-sharing internals, including Scheduling Cycle Refill (#13729 / #14605) and AFS accounting (#14153 / #15000).
