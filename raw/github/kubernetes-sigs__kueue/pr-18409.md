#### What this PR does / why we need it?
`(*JobSet).ReclaimablePods` currently reports succeeded child `Job`s as reclaimable while other `ReplicatedJob`s or replicas are still running, even when `spec.failurePolicy` can still trigger `RestartJobSet` (or `RestartJobSetAndIgnoreMaxRestarts`). If a running child `Job` fails, `RestartJobSet` recreates all child `Job`s—causing `ClusterQueue` over-admission once reclaimed quota was lent out, and wedging `ReconcileGenericJob` with `403 Forbidden` (`status.reclaimablePods[...]: cannot be removed`) before `WaitForPodsReady` checks.

Hold back partial `ReclaimablePods` updates on non-finished `JobSet`s while an incomplete `ReplicatedJob` can still trigger a whole-`JobSet` restart, and keep non-scaled-down `Workload.status.reclaimablePods` entries monotonic while holding quota so in-flight `JobSet`s across controller upgrades do not fail webhook validation. Partial reclaim remains immediate when `failurePolicy` is unset, `maxRestarts` is exhausted, or remaining stages use `FailJobSet`/`RestartJob` rules.

Fixes #17213
Related: #14811

#### Useful notes for your reviewer
- Covered unit tests in `jobset_controller_test.go` and `reconciler_test.go`, plus `envtest` integration tests in `test/integration/singlecluster/controller/jobs/jobset/jobset_controller_test.go` (scheduler over-admission guard + post-budget reclaim, `training + eval` stage reclaim with `TargetReplicatedJobs`, and pre-existing `reclaimablePods` upgrade reconciliation with `WaitForPodsReady`).
- AI assistance: Gemini assisted with root-cause analysis and drafting tests; all changes were reviewed and verified locally.

#### Release note
```release-note
JobSet: Avoid reporting completed ReplicatedJobs in Workload `status.reclaimablePods` while an incomplete ReplicatedJob can still trigger `RestartJobSet` or `RestartJobSetAndIgnoreMaxRestarts` (preventing `ClusterQueue` over-admission and `403` webhook errors on whole-JobSet restarts). Partial pod reclaim now waits until `maxRestarts` is exhausted or all ReplicatedJobs succeed, unless remaining stages use `FailJobSet`/`RestartJob` rules targeting those stages.
```

  `@MaysaMacedo` Yes. This is a defensive case, not one I can show occurs in a normal JobSet workflow.
  
  One possible sequence is:
  
  1. A JobSet has one configured replica and a `RestartJobSet` policy with restart budget remaining. Its Workload holds quota.
  2. Another actor creates an **extra** Job with the JobSet as its controller owner and valid current restart and replicated-job labels. The configured Job succeeds, and the extra Job fails.
  3. Before the JobSet is marked complete, its controller observes both Jobs in one reconciliation. It counts the configured replica as succeeded, but processes the extra failed Job before the success policy. It publishes `RestartingJobSet=True` with `Succeeded=1`.
  4. If Kueue observes that status before the next JobSet update, `allSucceeded` is true, so `ReclaimablePods` reports the replica as reclaimable. The JobSet controller can then recreate it after the restart.
  
  The JobSet v0.13.0 source shows the [child Job lookup and status calculation](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/jobset/blob/v0.13.0/pkg/controllers/jobset_controller.go#L482-L573) and the [failure-before-success policy order](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/jobset/blob/v0.13.0/pkg/controllers/jobset_controller.go#L252-L269).
  
  This requires an externally introduced extra Job and specific timing. I have not reproduced it in a test, and I do not have an example where the JobSet controller alone creates that state. The proposed change is therefore defensive against that unusual state.
