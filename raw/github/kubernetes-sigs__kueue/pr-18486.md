#### What this PR does / why we need it?

With `CustomMetricLabels` enabled, relabeling a ClusterQueue under the same parent Cohort left `kueue_cohort_subtree_admitted_active_workloads` on the old label series until the next workload event. The Cohort now keeps incremental per-label counts, so a relabel moves the count to the new series right away. An old series is deleted only once its last contributing ClusterQueue is gone, and recreating an implicit Cohort rebuilds counts from its children instead of wiping them.

Fixes #18234

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

This follows #18078 and starts fresh from main.

New tests in `pkg/cache/scheduler/cohort_metrics_test.go` fail on main and pass with the fix:
- relabeling a ClusterQueue moves its series before any workload event
- relabeling one of two ClusterQueues that share a label keeps the other's series
- relabeling a nested ClusterQueue updates the ancestor Cohort's series
- a `-race` test for admit/delete/report against relabel

Integration: "should move the cohort admitted-active series when a ClusterQueue is relabeled" in `test/integration/singlecluster/controller/core/custom_labels_test.go`.

Tested locally:
- `go test -race ./pkg/cache/scheduler/... ./pkg/metrics/...`
- `make test GO_TEST_TARGET=./pkg/cache`
- `INTEGRATION_TARGET=./test/integration/singlecluster/controller/core make test-integration` (111/111)
- `make fmt-verify`, golangci-lint on the touched packages

`make verify` didn't finish locally because `verify-skills-lint` needs docker.

Known limits, unchanged from today: deleting a workload still sets the series to 0 rather than deleting it, and relabeling a ClusterQueue with no admitted workloads can emit a 0-valued series.

I used an AI assistant to help write the fix and the tests; I reviewed and verified the change myself.
