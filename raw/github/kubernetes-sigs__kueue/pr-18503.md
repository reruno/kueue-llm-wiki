#### What this PR does / why we need it?
https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/14585 introduced the a new TAS check for Job-Workload equivalence. This causes problems for TAS rollbacks (by disabling the feature gate), as the workloads admitted when the gate was off will be stopped and rescheduled when the gate is enabled again.

This was cherry-picked to `release-0.19` in https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/16854, where it was observed by users rolling out TAS.

## AI summary

When `TopologyAwareScheduling` is enabled after a Job’s Workload was admitted without TAS, Kueue now ignores topology-request differences when checking whether the Job matches the Workload. This prevents Kueue from evicting the Workload and suspending the Job. Tests cover matching behavior and the feature-gate transition.

### Suggested release note

TAS: Fixed a bug that could evict an admitted Workload and suspend its Job when `TopologyAwareScheduling` was enabled after admission without TAS. Kueue now preserves the Workload and keeps the Job running.
