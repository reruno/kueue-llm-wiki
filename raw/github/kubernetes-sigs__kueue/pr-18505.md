#### What this PR does / why we need it?

## Why

When `ClassicalPreemptionCandidateOrderCache` is enabled, classical preemption
selects the next candidate by scanning every eligible ClusterQueue bucket.
Draining a segment with K buckets and L candidates costs O(K×L).

## What

Use a heap-based k-way merge for segments with four or more buckets. Smaller
segments keep the existing linear scan to avoid heap overhead. The change
preserves segment precedence, candidate ordering, and eligibility revalidation.

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

Benchmark results :

| ClusterQueues | Candidates per CQ | Linear scan | Heap iterator | Faster approach |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 145.6 ns/op | 300.3 ns/op | Linear scan |
| 2 | 1 | 443.7 ns/op | 605.2 ns/op | Linear scan |

| 16 | 1 | 19.26 µs/op | 6.09 µs/op | Heap iterator |

| 1 | 10 | 1.44 µs/op | 2.95 µs/op | Linear scan |
| 2 | 10 | 4.46 µs/op | 6.08 µs/op | Linear scan |
| 4 | 10 | 15.00 µs/op | 12.53 µs/op | Heap iterator |
| 100 | 10 | 7.78 ms/op | 0.45 ms/op | Heap iterator |

Segments with fewer than four ClusterQueue buckets retain the linear path.
