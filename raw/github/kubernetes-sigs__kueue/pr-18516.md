#### What this PR does / why we need it?
When Topology-Aware Scheduling (TAS) evaluates candidate domains below the root topology level (such as `required` or single-domain `preferred` at rack/subblock/hostname level, or `unconstrained`), candidate domains that tie on their own `affinityScore`, `sliceCount`, and `podCount` previously fell straight through to lexicographical `levelValues` comparison (`compareDomainLevelValues`).

Because parent/ancestor free capacity was ignored during tie-breaking, TAS would deterministically pick `"block-1"` over `"block-2"` even when `"block-2"` was smaller or already partially occupied:
- Suppose `block-1` has two free 8-GPU racks (`[8, 8]`, 16 GPUs total) and `block-2` has one free 8-GPU rack (`[8]`, 8 GPUs total).
- A `required: rack` 8-GPU workload sees three racks with identical capacity (`8 == 8 == 8`), picks `block-1/rack-1` alphabetically, and fragments `block-1` to 8 free GPUs.
- A subsequent `required: block` 12-GPU workload can then no longer be scheduled anywhere in the cluster, even though `block-2/rack-1` was an exact fit that would have left `block-1` intact.

This PR adds bottom-up ancestor free-capacity tie-breaking (`compareAncestorCapacity`) in `sortedDomains` and `sortedDomainsWithLeader` before `compareDomainLevelValues`:
- Walking from immediate `parent` up to the root (stopping early if candidates share a common ancestor), it prefers the candidate whose enclosing ancestor has smaller `sliceCount`, then smaller `podCount` (or leader-adjusted capacity when the candidate domains are leader-capable).
- Walking bottom-up packs already-fragmented intermediate domains (e.g., `subblock`) first, and steps up to higher levels (`block`) when intermediate domains tie.
- In Phase 2b multi-parent descent (`currentLevelIdx < sliceLevelIdx`), ancestor tie-breaking is skipped because `updateCountsToMinimumGeneric` has already overwritten parent `domainState`s with assigned counts and `BestFit` multi-block placement should keep pods co-located in the primary block.
- Guarded behind the `TASAncestorCapacityTieBreak` feature gate (Beta, enabled by default in `0.21`).

Related: #14850
