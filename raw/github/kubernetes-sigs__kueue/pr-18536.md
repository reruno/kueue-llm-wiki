#### What this PR does / why we need it?  
 
Breaks the `Reconcile` of the TAS topology ungater down into helpers, following the breakdowns in #17811 and #18520. `Reconcile` goes from 113 to 40 lines. The per-PodSet loop was about 55 lines nested three levels deep, and the ungate patch closure was inlined at the end.

| Helper | What moved |
|---|---|
| `podsToUngateForPodSet` | The per-PodSet loop body: listing Pods, resolving the rank offset, assigning gated Pods to domains and building the Pods to ungate |
| `podIndexOffsetFromAnnotation` | Reading and parsing the `PodIndexOffsetAnnotation` of a Pod |
| `withoutPodsOnUnhealthyNodes` | Leaving gated Pods that are assigned to a node from `Status.UnhealthyNodes` |
| `ungatePods` | Recording the expected UIDs and removing the scheduling gate from the Pods in parallel |

The lookup of the Workload, the skip checks and the expectations check stay in `Reconcile`, because they produce `wl`, `workloadSliceName` and `sliceKey` for the later steps.

#### Useful notes for your reviewer  
  
- The rank offset is now passed to `podsToUngateForPodSet` by value, and the parsed annotation value is no longer written back into the `rankOffsets` map. The map is built in `Reconcile` and is not read after the loop, and each key is read only by the iteration of the PodSet with that name, so the result is the same.
