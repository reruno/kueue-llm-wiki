#### What this PR does / why we need it:

Manual cherry-pick of #18516 to `release-0.19` (with `TASAncestorCapacityTieBreak` versioned at `0.19`).

When a workload requests a sub-tree topology domain (for example, `required: rack` in a `block -> rack -> host` hierarchy) and multiple candidate domains at that level have identical available capacity (`podCount` / `sliceCount`), `sortedDomains` and `sortedDomainsWithLeader` previously fell straight through to `slices.Compare(a.levelValues, b.levelValues)` — breaking ties alphabetically by domain label path (`["block-1", "rack-2"]` before `["block-2", "rack-1"]`) without considering the free capacity of their ancestor domains.

This PR adds a bottom-up ancestor free capacity tie-breaker (`compareAncestorCapacity`) between the target-level capacity checks and the final alphabetical `levelValues` tie-breaker, gated behind the `TASAncestorCapacityTieBreak` beta feature gate (enabled by default).

_Disclosure: AI assistance (Gemini) was used during drafting and test verification; all changes were reviewed and validated._
