#### What this PR does / why we need it?

`ClusterQueue.AddFromLocalQueue` pushed every Workload of the LocalQueue onto the heap without checking the requeue backoff, while `ClusterQueue.PushOrUpdate` parks a Workload as inadmissible until `requeueAt` passes and `Requeued` is not `False`. A Workload waiting for its backoff was therefore made available to the scheduler when its LocalQueue or ClusterQueue was added, for example when Kueue starts.

`AddFromLocalQueue` now inserts such a Workload as inadmissible, unless it is already tracked (active, inadmissible or inflight). Moving it to the heap when the backoff ends is unchanged: the workload controller updates `Requeued` / `requeueAt`, and `PushOrUpdate` reacts to that change.

Fixes #18653

## AI summary

Kueue could admit a Workload before its requeue backoff ended. This occurred when the Workload's LocalQueue or ClusterQueue was added, for example at Kueue startup. `ClusterQueue.AddFromLocalQueue` pushed each Workload to the active queue without checking the backoff.

This change checks the backoff first. If the backoff has not expired and the Workload is not tracked in the active, inadmissible, or inflight sets, Kueue inserts the Workload as inadmissible. Kueue does not add it to the active queue. Workloads with an expired backoff follow the existing active-queue path. A Workload moves to the active queue later through the existing requeue path, which updates `Requeued` and `requeueAt`. No feature gate controls this change.

Changed files:
- `pkg/cache/queue/cluster_queue.go`: adds the backoff check in `AddFromLocalQueue` (+8/-0).
- `pkg/cache/queue/cluster_queue_test.go`: extends `TestPendingResourcesAfterLocalQueueResync` with a requeue-backoff case (+16/-4).
- `pkg/cache/queue/manager_test.go`: adds `TestAddQueuesRespectRequeueBackoff`, which covers both LocalQueue and ClusterQueue addition orders (+60/-0).

The diff totals 3 files, 84 lines added, and 4 lines removed.

The author reports that the new unit tests fail without the fix and pass with it. The author also reports that `go test ./pkg/cache/queue/... -race -count=1` and `DOCKER=podman make verify` pass. These results were not verified here. The fixed build was not tested on a cluster.
