#### What this PR does / why we need it?

The concepts docs describe Fair Sharing only for flat cohorts. Two algorithms that matter in a CohortTree are not documented: how Kueue orders admission by comparing share values level by level (the tournament in `fair_sharing_iterator.go`), and how fair-sharing preemption walks down the tree to choose the target ClusterQueue (`fairsharing/ordering.go`).

This PR updates `site/content/en/docs/concepts/preemption/_index.md`:
- "ClusterQueue share value" becomes "Share value" and also covers the share value of a Cohort: what it summarizes, how a `lendingLimit` affects it, and where to read it (status and `kueue_cohort_weighted_share`). The formula stays in KEP-1714, which is linked.
- New "Admission order" section, with the order of preferences at each Cohort, the feature gates that change it, and a worked example.
- The "Algorithm overview" pseudocode now walks the CohortTree. The default behaviors controlled by `FairSharingPreemptWithinNominal`, `FairSharingReevaluatePreemptionCandidates` and `FairSharingVerifyFinalTargets` are listed after it.
- New "Choosing the target ClusterQueue" section, with two worked examples. The second one shows that Kueue does not preempt from a Cohort that is within its quota as a whole, even if one of its ClusterQueues borrows from a sibling.

`fair_sharing.md` gets a one-sentence summary under "Preemption based Fair Sharing", like the Admission Fair Sharing entry.

Fixes #7874

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

No behavior change (docs/typo).

- Every statement and every number in the three examples was checked against the code on main, including the per-ClusterQueue ordering from #18626 (a Workload waiting for its second pass is proposed before the head).
- The second preemption example is the case most likely to surprise users: `r-train` borrows 7 CPUs from `r-eval`, but `research` as a whole is within its quota, so a Workload from `prod` cannot preempt it.
- The existing proof in `fair_sharing.md` and the "Preemption algorithms" summary are unchanged.

- `site/content/en/docs/concepts/fair_sharing.md`: The "Preemption based Fair Sharing" description states that admission ordering and preemption-target selection use ClusterQueue and Cohort share values. These values provide equal or weighted shares of borrowable resources.
- `site/content/en/docs/concepts/preemption/_index.md`:
  - Renames the "ClusterQueue share value" heading to "Share value."
  - Defines Cohort share values and related metrics.
  - Describes CohortTree-based admission ordering, level-by-level comparison, and the related feature-gate preferences, with an admission-order example.
  - Updates the target-selection pseudocode. A candidate is allowed when the target queue is the preempting queue, when the preemptor-target AlmostLCA is within quota, or when the candidate satisfies a preemption strategy. Targets are discarded if the preemptor still does not fit.
  - Describes default preemption behavior, target-queue traversal through the CohortTree, candidate eligibility, and example selections.

This addresses the documentation request in issue `#7874` for the hierarchical cohort scheduling and preemption algorithms.
