#### What this PR does / why we need it?

A ClusterQueue can keep admitting other workloads while one workload remains pending for hours. Pending counts do not expose its age, and admission wait histograms only observe workloads after admission.

Add `kueue_oldest_pending_workload_age_seconds` to report the oldest current wait for quota reservation per ClusterQueue. Refresh it every five seconds even without queue activity, including active, inadmissible, and inflight pending workloads. Empty queues report zero; deleted queues and old replica-role series follow existing cleanup paths. Persisted creation, requeue, and post-eviction quota-release timestamps preserve the wait across restarts without including the preceding execution.

The metric uses ClusterQueue custom labels and `replica_role`. Workloads that already reserve quota and are waiting on admission checks are outside its scope. Documentation includes a four-hour alert example.

Fixes #18714

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

Related: #10124 and closed, unmerged PR #10633. That proposal exposed pending-wait quantiles; this change exposes only the maximum current age, which cannot be diluted by newly queued workloads. The scan is O(number of pending workloads) every five seconds and during explicit gauge resynchronization, rather than on every workload mutation. The periodic scan cost is a tradeoff for review.
