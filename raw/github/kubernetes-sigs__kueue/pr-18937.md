  AI review: If a ClusterQueue references a MultiKueue AdmissionCheck that has not been created yet, this lookup treats the Workload as local and blocks it when its DeviceClass is missing. Creating the AdmissionCheck later does not wake the blocked Workload: it has been removed from the scheduling queue, and status-only ClusterQueue updates do not trigger reconciliation. Reconcile affected pending DRA Workloads when the referenced AdmissionCheck becomes available, or retry while its identity is unresolved, and cover this creation-order case.
