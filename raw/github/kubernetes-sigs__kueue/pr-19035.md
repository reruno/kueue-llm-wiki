#### Useful notes for your reviewer

The cherry-pick had 23 conflicts on `release-0.19`:
- `multikueue/capacity_provider_test.go`, `controller/core/clusterqueue_custom_metric_labels_test.go`, `controller/jobs/job/job_controller_partial_scaleup_test.go`, `controller/jobs/job/job_controller_tas_replacement_test.go`, `controller/jobs/leaderworkerset/leaderworkerset_controller_test.go`, `scheduler/configurablepreemptions/suite_test.go`, `scheduler/fairsharing/refill_test.go`, `webhook/core/capacityprovider_test.go`, `webhook/core/dynamicquotaorchestrator_test.go`, `webhook/core/elastic_partial_scaleup_test.go`, `webhook/core/multikueuecapacityproviderconfig_test.go`, `webhook/core/preemptionconfig_test.go`: these files do not exist on the branch, because the tests they hold were not backported. I dropped them.
- `multikueue/external_job_test.go`, `multikueue/kuberay_test.go`, `multikueue/pod_test.go`, `multikueue/reconnect_test.go`, `multikueue/setup_test.go`, `webhook/jobs/deployment_webhook_test.go`, `webhook/jobs/raycluster_webhook_test.go`: the lines next to the `StartManager` call differ on the branch, because they use options, feature gates or setup that are only in `main` (`WithWorkerLostTimeout`, `managerCachedClient`, `DQOMultiKueueCapacityProvider`, `MultiKueueClusterProfile`, the inline `core.SetupControllers` call, `integrationManager`). I kept the branch's lines and applied the `StartManagerForSpec` rename.
- `controller/core/custom_labels_test.go`: the cohort relabel spec, the `anno1` workload annotation label and the LQ pending-metrics assertions do not exist on the branch. I kept the branch's specs. The `startManager` helper and its conversions applied to every spec on the branch.
- `controller/core/workload_hash_freshness_test.go`: the `Pending scheduling hashes under differing LimitRange defaults` container does not exist on the branch. I dropped it. The other container is converted as in `main`.
- `webhook/core/workload_test.go`: the `Workload topology-spreading validation` container does not exist on the branch. I dropped it and kept the branch's v1beta1 CEL specs.
- `controller/jobs/job/job_controller_test.go`: the only change from #18707 is in the `Job controller with waitForPodsReady unscheduledTimeout` container, and `UnscheduledTimeout` does not exist on the branch. I dropped it, so this file has no change.

The other 61 files applied cleanly, so the change covers 71 of the 84 files in #18707.

`make verify` passed. Integration tests: 844/844 specs passed across the 16 affected suites (singlecluster `controller/admissionchecks/provisioning`, `controller/concurrentadmission`, `controller/core`, `controller/dra`, `controller/jobframework/setup`, `controller/jobs/job`, `controller/jobs/statefulset`, `scheduler/delayedadmission`, `scheduler/fairsharing`, `scheduler/inadmissible`, `scheduler/podsready`, `webhook/core`, `webhook/jobs`, and `multikueue`, `multikueue/scheduler`, `multikueue/tas`).
