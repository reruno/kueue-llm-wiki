#### What this PR does / why we need it?

Simplifies error handling in `TestReconcileGenericJob`, as requested in
https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/pull/19111#discussion_r4624773916:

- `wantErr` is now an `error` compared with `cmp.Diff`, instead of a `func(error) bool` predicate.
- The two error-check branches are merged into one `cmp.Diff`.
- The requeue check no longer depends on `workloadCreateErr` and `wantErr`. Every case now expects an empty `Result`.

`workloadCreateErr` stays a separate field. It is the error injected into the Workload `Create` call, and that is not the error `ReconcileGenericJob` returns: an `AlreadyExists` is swallowed and the reconcile returns `nil`.

Fixes #19113

#### Useful notes for your reviewer

Test-only change. Coverage is unchanged: the swallowed `AlreadyExists` cases (Job and elastic Job), the no-requeue result, and the missing-namespace `NotFound` error.
