#### What this PR does / why we need it?

Fixes https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/kueue/issues/19325

With MultiKueue autoscaling, a scale-up slice can be replaced on the worker before the worker Job adopts it. In the [failed periodic run](https://prow.k8s.io/view/gs/kubernetes-ci-logs/logs/periodic-kueue-test-e2e-multikueue-extended-ray-autoscaling-main/2123938416771993600), on worker1:

1. Slice 1 is admitted. The manager repoints the worker RayJob to slice 1.
2. About 100ms later, slice 2 is admitted and replaces slice 1, so slice 1 is finished with `WorkloadSliceReplaced`. The RayJob has not adopted slice 1 yet and still points at it, because the manager repoints it to slice 2 only after slice 2 is admitted.
3. The RayJob reconciler skips slice 1 because of the check from kubernetes-sigs/kueue#16994 (a finished workload the Job does not own). It finds no workload and stops the RayJob (`Stopped: missing workload`).
4. KubeRay deletes the RayCluster, so the running Ray job is lost.

Now, when the prebuilt slice of a Job was replaced and an admitted slice of the same chain replaces it, the reconciler does not stop the Job. Until the MultiKueue manager repoints the Job to the new slice, the reconcile is retried with backoff. Without an admitted replacement, including when the new slice is evicted or deleted before the repoint, the Job is stopped as before.
