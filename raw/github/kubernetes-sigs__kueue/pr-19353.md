#### Useful notes for your reviewer

The cherry-pick had seven conflicts on `release-0.20`:
- `test/integration/singlecluster/controller/jobs/job/job_controller_partial_scaleup_test.go` and `test/integration/singlecluster/controller/jobs/sparkapplication/sparkapplication_controller_partial_scaleup_test.go`: these files do not exist on the branch, I dropped them.
- `pkg/controller/jobs/job/job_controller_test.go`: the partial scale-up cases in `TestPodSetsInfo` and `TestPodSets` do not exist on the branch, I kept the branch's version of those places. Six `TestReconciler` cases on the branch also enable `AssignQueueLabelsForPods` in the same `featureGates` map, so I removed only the `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlices` entry there.
- `pkg/controller/jobs/raycluster/raycluster_webhook_test.go` and `pkg/controller/jobs/rayjob/rayjob_webhook_test.go`: the `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlicesWithTAS` topology cases do not exist on the branch, I kept the branch's version.
- `pkg/controller/jobs/sparkapplication/sparkapplication_controller_test.go`: the stale workload annotation cases do not exist on the branch, I kept the branch's version, so this file has no changes.
- `pkg/controller/jobs/sparkapplication/sparkapplication_webhook_test.go`: the `ElasticJobsViaWorkloadSlicesForSparkApplication` gate and its cases are not on the branch, I kept the branch's version.

`go test -count=1` on the 23 changed `pkg/` packages passed. Integration tests: 1069/1069 specs passed across the 18 suites selected by `INTEGRATION_TARGET` for the 9 changed singlecluster packages, and `make test-multikueue-integration` passed with 99/99 specs across 3 suites. `make ci-lint` passed.
