#### Useful notes for your reviewer


With Fair Sharing, the second rule (LessThanInitialShare, rule S2-b) offers one candidate of a ClusterQueue and then drops the ClusterQueue. That fits a candidate that is evicted. In the shrink pass a candidate keeps its floor, or all its Pods if it cannot shrink, so its ClusterQueue may still borrow more than the preemptor. The other candidates of that ClusterQueue were never offered, the shrinks were not enough, and the search evicted a Workload.

Two examples with the default strategies. A row is a ClusterQueue with its nominal quota in brackets and one letter for each Pod, of one CPU. A capital letter is a Pod below the floor of its Workload.

```
  a [1]  .          <- in: 2 CPUs
  b [3]  PPp QQq
  c [2]  ..
```

Only p was offered, shrinking it was not enough, and p was evicted. Now p and q shrink to 2 Pods each. The same holds when p was already asked to shrink to 2 Pods.

```
  a [2]  ..         <- in: 3 CPUs
  b [3]  FF Eee
  c [1]  .
```

F cannot shrink. It was offered first, used up the rule for b, and was evicted. Now E shrinks to 1 Pod.

In the shrink pass, rule S2-b goes back to choosing the ClusterQueue after each offer, instead of dropping the ClusterQueue. It computes the shares again from what the plan left in the snapshot, and it makes progress because every offer takes a candidate off the list. The preemptionCtx tells the strategy that a shrink pass is walking the candidates (shrinkingPlan). The whole-eviction search does not change.

Tests that fail without the production change:

- TestPartialPreemption, with the default strategies:
  - "fair sharing: two victims of one ClusterQueue shrink, and neither is evicted": p is evicted and q is not a target, instead of both shrinking to 2 Pods.
  - "fair sharing: a second victim shrinks when the one already shrinking does not free enough": the same, p is evicted and q is not a target.
  - "fair sharing: a victim that cannot shrink is passed over, and the next one shrinks": fixed is evicted, instead of elastic shrinking to 1 Pod.
- TestScheduleForPartialPreemption, "fair sharing: two victims of one ClusterQueue shrink, and neither is evicted": the cycle evicts p and asks nothing of q, instead of asking both to shrink to 2 Pods.

The three cases of TestPartialPreemption also run with LessThanInitialShare as the only strategy. That passes with and without the change: the strategy then runs as the first rule, which does not drop a ClusterQueue after an offer.

## AI summary

Fixes partial preemption under Fair Sharing during the shrink pass. Rule S2-b now reconsiders a ClusterQueue after each offered candidate when a shrink pass is active. Multiple Workloads in one ClusterQueue can now shrink. The whole-eviction search is unchanged.

Changed files (source only, test files not inspected):
- `fair_strategy.go`: continues selecting from the ClusterQueue after a yield when `shrinkingPlan` is set.
- `partial_preemption.go`: sets and clears `shrinkingPlan` around the shrink walk.
- `preemption.go`: adds the `shrinkingPlan` field to `preemptionCtx`.

```text
FairSharing: Fixed a bug that caused partial preemption to shrink only one Workload per ClusterQueue when Fair Sharing is enabled. Now Kueue can shrink multiple Workloads in the same ClusterQueue when one shrink is not enough.
```
