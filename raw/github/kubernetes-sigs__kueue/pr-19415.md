#### What this PR does / why we need it?

Update `sigs.k8s.io/scheduler-library` to [its #75](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/scheduler-library/pull/75).

This provides support for _Pod-less victims_, i.e. workloads which may be preemption targets even though they don't have any Pods in the WAS ClusterSnapshot.

Such workloads may be e.g. non-TAS workloads, and even if they're "invisible" to WAS, handling them properly as preemption victims is necessary for a proper quota management on the Kueue side.

## AI summary

Pod-less workloads can now be preemption victims in the WAS scheduler simulator. Previously, `potentialVictims` and `committedVictims` skipped workloads with no Pods. Both now include them, so they can free quota and affect placement through the `PreemptionFilter`. The scheduler-library dependency is updated to a newer pseudo-version to support this.

## Suggested release note

```text
Scheduling: Fixed a bug where workloads without Pods, such as workloads waiting on AdmissionChecks that hold quota, could not be preempted. Kueue now considers these workloads as preemption victims.
```
